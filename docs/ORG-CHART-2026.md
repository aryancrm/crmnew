## What this means for "whose customer is it"

- Zoho does not say it on the customer. It says it on each sales order. Of 93,929 Zoho customers,
  25,755 ever ordered and **8,452** have a salesperson on an order; of the 5,889 who ordered since
  2025, all but one do. Division, Sub-division and Headquarter are on 1,380 customers' orders.
- The Hospital Division's Zoho names are the chart's seats one to one (24 seats, about 970
  customers). The others are tied by person or by district, as above.
- A customer nobody sold to through a named salesperson (most patients, and every Zoho customer who
  never ordered) is under no MedRep: Management and the Online desk see them, as they see every
  patient today.
- People who left (RENROSE, JM BULAN, MSA names) still hold customers in Zoho: those go to whoever
  holds that work now, which is the heads' call.
