# The sellers: how to do each task

This part is for everyone who sells: the **Hospital CHC**, the Hospital Division's **Government
Account** (its bidding coachee), the **Rx CHC**, **MD Telesales – Anesthesia**, **MD Telesales**
(Oncology), the B2B **NBD** and **CRR**, the **CLIDP Sales Coach**, **Government Bidding**, the
**Patient Assistance (PAP)** officer and **Modern Trade** (the COO, who looks after the chains).
Most of you work on your phone, so the steps say "tap".

Rules that hold on every seller's screen:

- **Management approves every order.** Yours goes to Management first, then Finance, then Dispatch.
- **One seat, one Division.** Your order carries your seat's Division. You do not pick another.
- **Rx for every patient purchase.** An order for a patient cannot be placed without the prescription.
- **A price below the list needs a reason.** Management decides on it.
- **You order for yourself only.** The order counts on your seat, your target and your lead.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Orders are cancelled, goods are returned and bills are credited; the history stays.
- **You collect; Finance records.** You chase the money and bring the proof. Finance records payments and makes invoices.

Screens below name a page path and the login to take the picture with, on the local copy. Logins
are seat IDs: `hos1` Hospital CHC, `hos27` Government Account, `rx1` Rx CHC, `ts1` MD Telesales –
Anesthesia, `ts6` MD Telesales (Oncology), `b2b4` NBD, `b2b3` CRR, `b2b1` CLIDP, `bid1` Government
Bidding, `pap1` PAP officer, `tl10` Modern Trade. Take phone pictures at phone width.

## Part A. Every seller

### Sign in on your phone
- **Who**: every seller.
- **When**: at the start of the day, or whenever the app shows the sign-in page.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open Bishnoi One in your phone's browser. The page says **Bishnoi One · Company portal** and shows a tile for each workspace.
  2. Tap the **Sales and field** tile (MedReps, team leads, heads).
  3. The box **Sign in to Sales and field** opens. Tap **Enter your login ID** and type your seat's login ID (for example `hos1`).
  4. Tap **Enter your login password** and type the company's common password. Tap the eye to see what you typed.
  5. Tap **Enter Sales and field**.
  6. To leave, tap the round button with your initials at the top right, then **Sign out**.
- **What happens next**: your own Home opens. The top bar names your seat ("Hospital CHC", "NBD · New Business", "Patient Assistance (PAP)"...).
- **Good to know**:
  - The login ID belongs to your seat, not to your name. If you change seat, you take the new seat's ID.
  - The password is the same for the whole company and is never forced to change. It is not written in this book: ask your lead. **Change password** is in the same initials menu if you ever need it.
  - No access yet? The page says **Need access? Ask your administrator**.
  - The tile you used last has an orange dot.
- **Screen**: `/login` signed out, phone width (the tiles); then `/login?dept=sales` with the sign-in box.

  - **Government Bidding**: Home · Tenders (Tenders, Documents) · My orders · Collections · My calls · Sales performance.

### Read your Home
- **Who**: every seller (the PAP officer's Home is the PAP cases list: see Part C).
- **When**: first thing in the morning, and between visits.
- **Steps**:
  1. Tap **Home**. The top says "Good morning, (your name)", your channel, your area and who you report to.
  2. **Hospital CHC and MD Telesales – Anesthesia**: read the four coloured tiles at the top (today's pulse). Green is fine, amber needs a look, red needs action today.

     - **Days to collect**: how many days your customers take to pay. Tap it to open **Collections**.
     - **Not reordered**: customers late or at risk against their own usual gap between orders. Tap it to open the late list to call.

  3. Read **Go here first** under the tiles: up to six stops, numbered. First the hospitals due to reorder (biggest buyers first), then up to two who owe money 60 days and more. Each row has **Call** (rings their number), **New order** (opens a new order for them) or **Collect** (opens the order whose invoice is oldest).
  4. **MD Telesales (Oncology)**: your tiles are **Sales vs target**, **Refills due**, **Groups waiting** and **Invoice on the last working day**. Under them are the care groups waiting for first contact and **Refills due** (today and the next two days), each with **Call** and **New order**.
  5. **Rx CHC, NBD, CRR, CLIDP, Government Bidding**: your own boxes come first instead (see each in Part C).

  7. Read **My KPIs** (your scorecard this month so far). Tap **Open** for the whole of it.
  8. Under **Orders · Today**, a yellow line means an order was sent back to you; a red line means one is on hold. Tap **Fix** or **Open**.
  9. On the right (under, on a phone): **My customers** with tabs **Hospitals**, **Doctors**, **Patients** (not on B2B seats), **My orders right now** (a ring of where your open orders are), **Tonight's huddle** (Hospital CHC only), **To collect** (other seats), **Target** and **Stock news** (out of stock, low stock, back in stock).
- **What happens next**: every number opens the list behind it. Nothing is changed from Home.
- **Good to know**:
  - Home refreshes by itself every minute or two.
  - "Go here first" says "No hospital due to reorder and nothing owed past 60 days. Plan new hospitals." when the day is clear.
  - Your team lead and head see the same figures for your seat.
- **Screen**: `/` as `hos1` (pulse, Go here first, huddle card); `/` as `ts6` for the Oncology tiles. Scroll to **My orders** for the period boxes.

### Create a new order: start, and part 1 (General)
- **Who**: every seller with **Create New Order** on top of the menu (all seats in this part).
- **When**: a customer agrees to buy.
- **Steps**:
  1. Tap **Create New Order** at the top of the menu (on the phone, the green **+ New order** in the bottom bar). To order for a customer you are looking at, tap **New order for them** on their page, or **New order** beside them on Home or a list: the customer is then filled in already.
  2. The page **Create New Order** opens: "Fill the five parts. Management approves it, then Finance and Dispatch take it on."
  3. Look at the strip at the top: **General**, **Customer**, **Medicines**, **Documents**, **Review and place**. A red number says how many things are still missing in that part; a green tick says it is done. Tap a part to jump to it. "N of 5 ready" counts them.
  4. In **1 · General**, check **Order date** (today, filled by itself) and **MedRep** (you, your channel and area: "Your own account: the order is yours, in your area.").
  5. Check **Division**. It is shown as one green chip: your seat's Division. The line under it says "Your Division: the order goes to your own team lead and head, and counts for you."
  6. If you sit in two Divisions, both show as buttons: tap the one this order is for.
  7. Check **Sub-division**. It fills itself from your area (for B2B: NBD or CRR from your seat). Change it only if it is wrong.
- **What happens next**: go on to part 2. The customer list in part 2 opens on your Division's usual buyers (for example "HOS orders are usually for hospitals and doctors").
- **Good to know**:
  - One seat, one Division: you cannot put your order under another Division. The system refuses it.
  - You place orders for yourself only. There is no "for another MedRep".
- **Screen**: `/orders/new` as `hos1`, top of the page with the step strip and **1 · General**.

### Create a new order: part 2 (Customer details)
- **Who**: every seller.
- **When**: right after part 1.
- **Steps**:
  1. In **2 · Customer details**, you see the five customers you ordered for lately, under "or one you ordered for lately". Tap one to pick it.
  2. Or type in **Search by name, phone number or city**. The list shows your Division's usual buyers. Tap **Every customer** (or "N more of other kinds: show every customer") to see the rest.
  3. Not there? Tap **New customer** at the top (next task).
  4. Read the customer's card: **In your area** or **Not in your area**, how they pay, their licence, and **New customer, not checked yet** if Management has not checked them. A red box warns if they owe money past due or this order takes them over their credit limit: "Management sees it before approving: collect first if you can."
  5. Fill the fields under the card:
     - **Delivery address** (filled from the customer; change it if the goods go elsewhere).
     - **Receiver name** and **Receiver contact no.** (if someone else receives).
     - **Payment terms**: leave "The customer's usual: …", or pick from the list (PDC 30, NET 30, COD…), or **Other: number of days…** ("… days after delivery"), or **By a date…** ("Pays by").
     - **Source**: where the order came from (Doctor order, Hospital PO, Distributor order, Bidding Order…).
     - **Invoicing from**: **2MG Inc.** or **GetMeds Philippines Inc.**
     - **Hospital PO number** (or **Customer's PO number**) if they gave one.
     - **Date needed**: today or later.
     - **Doctor (if any)**: type in **Search a doctor**; for a hospital, its doctors are listed under "Doctors at this hospital".
     - **Customer remarks**: what the customer asked and anything Management should know.
  6. To change the customer, tap **Change** beside their name.
- **What happens next**: go on to part 3. Missing fields show on the strip at the top.
- **Good to know**:
  - Needed fields have a red star: Delivery address, Source, Date needed and Customer remarks must be filled before you can place the order.
  - A customer is known by their phone number: one number, one customer.
  - The customer receives an email at each step of the order when their email is on file.
- **Screen**: `/orders/new` as `hos1`, scrolled to **2 · Customer details** with a hospital picked (the red box shows on one that owes past due).

### Add a new customer while placing an order
- **Who**: every seller.
- **When**: the buyer is not in the CRM yet.
- **Steps**:
  1. In **2 · Customer details**, tap **New customer** ("A hospital, doctor or patient not in the CRM yet. Their phone number is checked first.").
  2. The box **Add a customer** opens. Under **Kind**, tap the kind of buyer (it starts on your Division's usual one).
  3. Type the **Phone** first. If the number or name is already on file, a yellow box says **Already on file? Check before adding again.** Tap **Use this one** to take that customer instead of adding a second one.
  4. Type the name (for example **Hospital's name**), **City**, and **Email**. No email? Tick **No email**.
  5. For a hospital, drugstore or distributor, fill **Licence and papers**: **TIN**, **Licence owner**, **LTO number**, **LTO type**, **Issued on**, **Valid until**, and tap **Attach** on **BIR 2303**, **LTO permit** and **Business permit** to add a photo of each.
  6. Tap **More details** for the address, a doctor's **Specialty** and **PRC licence no.**, or a **Note**.
  7. Tap the green **Add hospital** (or **Add patient**, **Add drugstore**…).
- **What happens next**: the customer is picked on your order at once. They show as "New: Management checks it" until Management looks them over.
- **Good to know**:
  - A hospital can only be added with all its papers ("A hospital can't be added yet. Missing: …").
  - A drugstore or distributor with something missing can still be added: tick **I'll follow up on the missing ones: add it anyway (Management reviews it)**.
  - "You can use this customer at once. Management looks the details over afterwards."
  - Missing papers can be added later on the customer's page with **Edit details**.
- **Screen**: `/orders/new` as `b2b4`, tap **New customer**, kind **Drugstore**: the box with **Licence and papers**.

### Create a new order: part 3 (Medicines)
- **Who**: every seller.
- **When**: after the customer is picked.
- **Steps**:
  1. In **3 · Medicines**, tap **Select a medicine (N in the list)** and type part of its name. Tap it to add it.
  2. Not in our list? Tap **Ask Inventory for it**. In **Medicine not available**, check the **Medicine**, type the **Strength**, **How many (pieces)** and **For which customer**, and tap **Send to Inventory**. Inventory answers there; the order cannot carry that medicine until it is in the list.
  3. On the medicine's card, read its VAT and the red **Prescription** tag if it needs one. A red line says **Out of stock** or **Not all of it is in stock** ("Dispatch sees it"), with the day more arrive when known.
  4. Under "Price for one piece · tap the one you are giving", tap the price you are giving. The customer's own kind of price comes first (for a hospital, **Hospital**), then any **Promo to (date)** or offer.
  5. Set the quantity with **−** and **+**, or type it. It starts at one pack.
  6. **Giving a lower (or higher) price?** Tap **Give another price**. Type the price in **You gave it at (₱ each)** and the reason in **Why** (for example "Hospital asked to match their last order"). The box says how much below the list it is and "Management decides." Tap **Use the price** to go back to the list price.
  7. **Free goods**: tap **+ Free (a sample, a deal)**. A **Free** line at ₱0 is added under the medicine. Type the **Promo** (Buy 1 Take 1, Buy 2 Take 1, Sample…) and the **Free pieces**. When an offer is running on the medicine, its free pieces are added by themselves ("Buy 1 Take 1 · +N free, added by itself").
  8. **Senior citizen or PWD?** Tap **Senior citizen** or **PWD** if the buyer shows that ID. Type the **OSCA ID number** (or **PWD ID number**) and the **Name on the ID**. Every paid medicine then takes the patient price without VAT and 20% off (or a lower promo without VAT, never both).
  9. Remove a medicine with the **x** on its card.
- **What happens next**: the total at the bottom of the screen updates as you go. Go on to part 4.
- **Good to know**:
  - Prices come from Inventory. If a medicine shows "No price set for this medicine yet. Ask Management.", it cannot be sold until a price is set.
  - A lower price without a reason stops the order ("Why the price is different" is missing).
  - A medicine short on the shelf can still be ordered; you are asked why when you place it.
  - With the senior or PWD price, add a photo of their ID in part 4: Management checks it.
- **Screen**: `/orders/new` as `hos1`, a hospital picked, one medicine with **Give another price** open, and one **Free** line.

### Create a new order: part 4 (Documents)
- **Who**: every seller.
- **When**: after the medicines.
- **Steps**:
  1. In **4 · Documents**, for a hospital or business: answer **Is this order for one patient?** Tap **Yes, a patient** if the hospital or doctor is buying for one patient, and type **The patient's name**.
  2. The list of papers follows the buyer. For a patient (or "Yes, a patient"): **Prescription** (marked **Needed**), **Patient's ID**, **Senior citizen / PWD ID**, **Guarantee letter**, **Customer's PO**, **Proof of payment**. For a business: **Customer's PO**, **Their papers (LTO, accreditation…)**, **Prescription**, **Proof of payment**.
  3. Tap **Add photo** beside each paper you have. Take the photo or pick a PDF.
  4. **Proof of payment**: add the slip if they paid. If there is none yet, pick why in **No proof of payment? Say why…**: **Customer is on payment terms**, **Payment to follow**, **Paid, no slip yet** or **Other**. For **Other**, type what in **Say what (needed)**; otherwise **A word for Finance (optional)**.
  5. To remove a photo you added by mistake, tap the **x** beside it.
- **What happens next**: the photos are sent with the order when you place it. Go on to part 5.
- **Good to know**:
  - Rx for every patient purchase: without the prescription, "Prescription" stays missing and the order cannot be placed.
  - A customer on credit or cash on delivery starts with "Customer is on payment terms" picked.
  - A customer who pays in advance with no slip is flagged to Management: "No proof of payment yet, and this customer pays in advance".
  - A paper that fails to upload can be added again on the order's page.
- **Screen**: `/orders/new` as `ts6` with a patient picked: **Prescription** marked **Needed** and the "No proof of payment?" list.

### Create a new order: part 5 (Review and place)
- **Who**: every seller.
- **When**: all parts are filled.
- **Steps**:
  1. In **5 · Review and place**, check the totals: **Subtotal**, **Lower price given**, **VAT** ("Included in the prices") and **Total**.
  2. Read the yellow box **Your part ends here. Management, Finance and Dispatch do the rest.** Each line is something Management will see (a price below the list, a new customer, an expired licence, short stock). "Nothing for Management to question." means all is clean.
  3. If a red **Missing:** line shows, tap the red **N to fill** button at the bottom: it takes you to the first missing part.
  4. When it says **Everything needed is filled. Place it to see the summary first.**, tap **Place order** at the bottom.
  5. The window **Place this order?** shows the customer, Division, how they pay, source, date needed, address, company invoicing and documents, then each medicine and the total.
  6. If a medicine is short on the shelf, answer **Why are you proceeding?** (for example "The hospital waits for the next delivery"). Management and Dispatch read it.
  7. Tap **Confirm and place**. (Tap **Go back** to change something.)
- **What happens next**: the order's page opens with its number (2MG-…) and the status **Pending Management Approval**. Management checks it (papers, price, stock, customer and licence) and approves it, holds it, or sends it back. Then Finance checks the payment or what the customer owes and makes the invoice, and Dispatch packs and ships it. You are told at each step in **Messages**.
- **Good to know**:
  - Management approves every order, whatever it says on the yellow box.
  - If a photo did not upload, the order page says so: add it again under the order's papers.
  - A PAP officer who picks a patient is sent to **Start a PAP case** instead: a patient on a guarantee letter starts as a PAP case (Part C).
- **Screen**: `/orders/new` as `hos1`, all parts filled, the **Place this order?** window open.

### Save an order as a draft and finish it later
- **Who**: every seller.
- **When**: you must stop halfway (the customer is still deciding, the signal is weak).
- **Steps**:
  1. On **Create New Order**, tap **Save draft** at the top right.
  2. A line says "Draft saved on this device at (time). Photos aren't kept in a draft."
  3. Later, tap **Create New Order** again on the same phone. The line says "Draft from (time) brought back." and everything you typed is back.
  4. Add the photos again in part 4, then place the order.
  5. To start clean instead, tap **Discard draft**.
- **What happens next**: nothing goes to Management until you place the order. A draft is not an order.
- **Good to know**:
  - The draft lives on that phone only. It does not show on another phone or computer, and nobody else sees it.
  - You keep one draft at a time. Placing the order clears it.
  - Opening a new order from a customer's **New order** button does not bring the draft back.
- **Screen**: `/orders/new` as `hos1` after **Save draft** (the grey line with **Discard draft**).

### Follow an order ("Where the order is")
- **Who**: every seller.
- **When**: a customer asks where their order is, or you check your day.
- **Steps**:
  1. Tap **My orders**. The tabs are **All**, **Sent back to me**, **With Management**, **With Finance**, **Packing or on the way**, **On hold** and **Delivered this month**, each with its count. Orders are grouped by the day you placed them. **Returns** is at the end of the tabs.
  2. Tap an order's card. Its page opens: **Order ID**, the customer, how they pay, the status and the total. One line under it shows the address, the warehouse it ships from, the invoice number (or "made when approved"), the Division and the PO. Tap **More details** for the rest.
  3. On the left (on top, on a phone) is **Where the order is**: 1 **Placed**, 2 **Management check**, 3 **Finance check**, 4 **Pack & ship**, 5 **Delivered**, 6 **Paid**. A green tick is done, a green ring is where it is now ("Now · Finance"), red is stopped.
  4. Tap a step to open it beside the list:
     - **Management check**: what Management checks and "Waiting for Management to read it and approve." or who approved it and when.
     - **Finance check**: "With Finance now…" and the payment papers.
     - **Pack & ship** and **Delivered**: the courier, the tracking number, who received it and the signed delivery receipt.
     - **Paid**: the invoice, what was received and what is still owed.
     - **Placed**: the order as you placed it (medicines, documents, returns).
  5. Under the steps, read the order's history (every step, with who and when) and the money card.
  6. To tell the customer, tap **Send to customer** at the top and pick Email, WhatsApp, Viber, SMS or **Copy text**. **Sales order** prints the sales order.
- **What happens next**: you are told in **Messages** when it moves on, is held, sent back or delivered.
- **Good to know**:
  - "Paid" is ticked only when the invoice is paid in full. A credit order is delivered first and paid later.
  - "Changed by Mgmt" on an order means Management changed it before approving; the page shows what and why.
- **Screen**: `/orders` as `hos1` (the tabs), then `/orders/(an order of hos1's)` with **Where the order is** on the left.

### Fix an order Management sent back
- **Who**: every seller.
- **When**: Management sends your order back (the order shows **Sent back to me**, Home shows a yellow line, and **Messages** tells you).
- **Steps**:
  1. Open it from Home ("… was sent back. … **Fix**"), from **My orders** › **Sent back to me** (**Fix and send again** on the card), or from the order page.
  2. On the order page, read the yellow band: "Sent back by (name) — What to fix: (reason)".
  3. To change the order, tap **Fix and resubmit**. The form **Fix order 2MG-…** opens with everything filled in and Management's reason on top.
  4. Change what is needed: medicines, prices and reasons, address, terms, date, papers. Papers already on the order show "On the order already".
  5. At the bottom, type **Your answer to Management** ("What you changed, or why it is right as it is"). It is needed.
  6. Tap **Send to Management**, then **Send to Management** again in the window **Send it to Management again?**
  7. If nothing needs changing, tap **↩ Resubmit as it is** on the order page instead, type **Your answer to Management** and tap **Send to Management**.
- **What happens next**: the order goes back to Management's queue as **Pending Management Approval**, with your answer on it. Management sees what you changed.
- **Good to know**:
  - The customer and the Division cannot be changed in a fix. For another customer, cancel this order and place a new one.
  - A sent-back order can also be cancelled: tap **Cancel order** under **Where the order is** and say why. It is kept, not deleted.
- **Screen**: `/orders/(a sent-back order)` as `hos1` with the yellow band, then `/orders/(same)/fix` (the **Fix order** form).

### Answer a hold, or cancel your order
- **Who**: every seller.
- **When**: an order of yours is **On hold** (Management, Finance or Dispatch held it with a reason).
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the order. A red band shows **On Hold:** and the reason. Finance's screenshots show under it when Finance held it.
  2. If the hold is for a missing slip or paper, add it: open the step **Finance check** (or **Placed**) and tap **Add photo** beside the paper.
  3. To answer, tap **↩ Re-submit**. In **Answer the hold**, type **Your answer** and tap **Send the answer**.
  4. To stop the order, tap **Cancel order**, say why and confirm.
- **What happens next**: your answer goes on the order and to the desk that held it. The hold stays until that desk takes it off. A Finance hold for a missing slip ends by itself when the proof of payment is added.
- **Good to know**:
  - You can cancel your own order only while it is sent back, or held before Finance has worked on it. After that, ask Management.
  - A cancelled order is kept with its reason. Management can restore it.
  - "Waiting for stock" holds end when the stock arrives; the page says "Stock is back".
- **Screen**: `/orders/(an order on hold)` as `hos1` with the red band and **↩ Re-submit**.

### Ask for a return of delivered goods
- **Who**: the seller whose order it is (or Management).
- **When**: a customer sends back delivered medicines (expired, damaged, wrong item, not needed), or keeps them but must be credited.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the delivered order. Tap **Request refund** at the top (or open step 1 **Placed** and tap **Return or refund** in **Returns**).
  2. The box **Return or refund** says "Where the goods are, what and why: Management decides." Pick where the goods are:
     - **Coming back**: "Management approves, Dispatch checks what came back, then Management credits the bill."
     - **The customer keeps them**: "Nothing comes back: the bill is credited when Management approves."
  3. Beside each medicine ("Up to N · ₱ each"), type how many come back.
  4. Pick **Why**: **Expired**, **Damaged**, **Wrong item**, **Not needed** or **Other**. Add **A word (optional)**.
  5. Tap **Ask Management**.
- **What happens next**:
  - The return gets a number and shows **Waiting for Management** in the order's **Returns** and on **My orders** › **Returns**.
  - Management approves it (or **Don't approve**, with why).
  - Coming back: it shows **Approved: Dispatch checks the goods**. Dispatch checks each line: good goes back on the shelf, damaged is written off, missing is not credited. It then shows **Goods checked: Management credits it**, and Management credits the bill (or not, with why).
  - The customer keeps them: the bill is credited when Management approves.

  6. Tick each **Envelope paper** as it is ready (PhilGEPS Platinum certificate, Omnibus Sworn Statement, bid security, technical specs and stock availability, FDA LTO, CPR of each medicine, NFCC, SLCC).
  7. Ask the Government Bidding Admin to approve the price (they tap **Price approved**).
  8. When all envelope papers are ticked, tap **Submitted** and **Mark submitted**.
- **What happens next**: the tender waits under **Awaiting award**. Its **History** lists every step with who and when.
- **Good to know**:
  - The price cannot be approved above the ABC, and the bid cannot be submitted with an envelope paper missing.
  - The bid price can be changed only before the price is approved.
  - The company's papers and their expiry are under **Tenders** › **Documents**.
  - A tender not pursued: tap **Dropped** and say why.
- **Screen**: `/tenders` as `bid1` with **Record a tender** open; `/tenders/(a tender)` as `bid1` with the envelope papers and bid lines.

### Government Bidding: after the award (order, delivery, acceptance, payment)
- **Who**: Government Bidding coachees and the Government Account.
- **When**: the result comes out.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the tender. Tap **Won** (or **Lost**). For **Lost**, type the **Winning bid (₱)** or why we lost. For **Won**, pick the day of the notice of award.
  2. Place the order for the goods with **Create New Order**: the agency or hospital as customer, **Source** **Bidding Order**, the PO number if any, and the contract papers in part 4.
  3. On the tender, tap **Contract / NTP** when the contract or notice to proceed comes.
  4. When the goods are delivered, tap **Delivered**. When the agency inspects and accepts them, tap **Accepted (IAR)**.
  5. Tap **Billed** when you bill them, and **Paid** when they pay. Each asks **On which day (today if empty)**.
- **What happens next**: the tender moves through **Awarded** to **Paid, lost, dropped**. Government money is owed from acceptance; it shows in **Collections** through the order's invoice.
- **Good to know**:
  - The tender and the order are not linked in the system. The tender's steps (Delivered, Billed, Paid) are marked by hand; the order's invoice and payment are Finance's, as for any order.
  - The government pays only after inspection and acceptance (IAR).
- **Screen**: `/tenders?view=awarded` as `bid1`; `/tenders/(a won tender)` as `bid1` with the **Mark …** box.

### PAP: open a PAP case for a patient with a guarantee letter
- **Who**: PAP officer.
- **When**: a patient will pay with a guarantee letter from DSWD, PCSO, OVP or the Office of the President.
- **Steps**:
  1. Tap **New PAP case** under **Create New Order** (or on Home, the **PAP cases** list, tap **New PAP case**).
  2. In **New PAP case**, type **Who is writing or calling**, pick **They are** (the patient, or their relation), and **The patient's name** if it is someone else.
  3. Type the **Phone**, **Email** (or tick **No email**), **Delivery address** and **City / province**. Pick **Came in through**.
  4. Under **Guarantee letter from**, tap the agency: **DSWD (AICS)**, **PCSO (IMAP)**, **Office of the Vice President** or **Office of the President (PMS)**.
  5. Tap **Open the case**. The case page opens.
  6. Step **1 Enquiry & quotation**: add the medicines and quantities, then **Save quotation** (it is the "Quotation for the agency").
  7. Step **1.1 (agency) application**: type **Applied on** and tap **Record the application**.
  8. Step **1.2 Guarantee letter**: add the **Photo of the letter**, type the **Letter number**, **Amount granted (₱)**, **Letter dated** and **Valid until**. Tap **Save**, then **Mark the letter verified**.
  9. Step **2 Rx & documents**: add the **Prescription**, the **Patient's valid ID** and the **Medical certificate or clinical abstract**.
- **What happens next**: the case moves along the PAP cases board (**New**, **Rx**, **Quote**, **Papers / GL**, **Ready**, **Ordered**, **Delivered, unpaid**, **Done**). The card shows "Papers N/N".
- **Good to know**:
  - A patient on a guarantee letter always starts as a PAP case. **Create New Order** with a patient sends you here ("A patient on a guarantee letter starts as a PAP case.").
  - If the letter grants less than the quotation, the page says so.
  - A case that ends without an order is closed as lost with why; it is kept.
- **Screen**: `/pap` as `pap1` (the cases list); `/pap?new=1` as `pap1` (the **New PAP case** box); a case page at step **1.2 Guarantee letter**.

### PAP: place the order, send the claim and follow the payment
- **Who**: PAP officer.
- **When**: the letter is verified and the papers are in.
- **Steps**:
  1. On the case, open step **3 Order & delivery**. Read "Once placed, it goes": **The PAP head approves** › **Finance checks the agency's bill** › **Dispatch packs and ships**, and the total **Billed to the agency**.
  2. Tap **Place the order**.
  3. Follow the order on the same step (or open it from **My orders**).
  4. After the hand-over, open step **4 Claim & payment**. In **4.1 Pick-up photo**, add the photo of the patient with the medicines.
  5. In **4.2 Claim sent to (agency)**, pick **Sent on**, type the **Claim reference (optional)** and tap **Claim sent**.
  6. **4.3 (agency) pays**: "Finance records the agency's money on the invoice when it comes."
  7. Under **Follow-up · next refill**, set the day the patient's medicines run out.
- **What happens next**: the order goes to the PAP head to approve, then Finance and Dispatch. The case shows **Delivered, unpaid** until Finance records the agency's money, then **Done**.
- **Good to know**: the PAP order is approved by the PAP head, not by the Management desk. Your Home is the PAP cases list.
- **Screen**: a PAP case as `pap1` at step **3 Order & delivery**, and at step **4 Claim & payment**.

### Modern Trade: the chains and their listings
- **Who**: Modern Trade (the COO).
- **When**: weekly, and when a chain lists or delists one of our medicines.
- **Steps**:
  1. On Home, read **Owed by the chains**, **Chains to call** and **Medicines listed**, then **The chains** (each with its last order and whether it is due or late), **Money owed by chain** and **Listings**.
  2. To record a listing, tap **Add or change** under **Listings** (Home or **Listings** in the menu).
  3. In **A chain's listing**, pick the **Chain** and the **Medicine**, type **Branches carrying it**, and keep **Listed** ticked (untick it when the chain delists it). Tap **Save**.
  4. Place the chain's purchase orders with **Create New Order** (part 4: the chain's PO).
- **What happens next**: the listing shows with its branches and since when. A change is kept as a new line.
- **Good to know**: the chains' deductions and near-expiry returns are not in the CRM yet. They wait for Finance's record of a short payment.
- **Screen**: `/` as `tl10`; `/mt/listings` as `tl10` with **A chain's listing** open.
