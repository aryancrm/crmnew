# Bishnoi One: how to do it

Everyone's basics, team leads and heads, HR, the CEO and the Admin. Each task is one procedure.
Labels are written exactly as the screen shows them, in **bold**.

---

## Part 1. Everyone's basics

### Signing in
- **Who**: everyone.
- **When**: at the start of the day, or whenever the app shows the company portal.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open Bishnoi One. The company portal opens: "One company. Every department, one place."
  2. Press the tile of your workspace: **Sales and field** (MedReps, team leads, heads), **Management**, **Finance**, **Dispatch**, **Inventory**, **Online desk**, **HR**, **Regulatory**, **Office staff**, **CEO**, **Administration** or **Books**. You can type in **Search workspaces** to find it. A small orange dot marks the tile you used last.
  3. The department's page opens with the sign-in box **Sign in to …** on it.
  4. In **Enter your login ID** type your seat's login ID (for example the one HR or the Admin gave you, like a short code with a number). Your work email also works if you were given one.
  5. In **Enter your login password** type the company's common password (ask HR or your team lead; it is the same for everyone). The eye button shows what you typed.
  6. Press **Enter …** (for example **Enter Sales and field**).
- **What happens next**: your own desk opens on its Home. What you see depends on your login, not on the tile you pressed.
- **Good to know**:
  - The login ID belongs to the seat, not to the person: whoever sits in the seat today signs in with it.
  - Five wrong passwords lock the login for 15 minutes ("Too many wrong passwords. Try again in … minutes.").
  - On the **Management**, **Finance** and **Dispatch** tiles there are two tabs: **Desk screen** (the desk's shared screen, company password only, no ID) and **My own login**. Use **My own login** to reach your own leave, payslips and messages; the shared screen has none of them.
  - The other way in: **Log in with ID and password** at the foot of the portal opens a plain sign-in; its button is **Open my workspace**.
  - Can't get in? "Need access? Ask your administrator."
- **Screen**: `/login`, signed out, top of page ("One company."); then `/login?dept=sales`, signed out, scroll to "Sign in to Sales and field".

### Starting and ending your working day
- **Who**: everyone with a login of their own.
- **When**: when you begin work, and when you stop.
- **Steps**:
  1. Look at the green button in the top bar, left of your initials.
  2. If it says **Start my day**, press it. It then shows **In since** and the time.
  3. To finish, press **In since …**. A small window opens: **Today**, your working hours, **In**, **Out**, **Hours** (and "Late … today" if you came late).
  4. Press **End my day**.
- **What happens next**: the button turns grey and shows **Done ·** with your hours. Your day goes into your attendance (the window's **My attendance** link opens your month).
- **Good to know**:
  - Your day also starts by itself with the first thing you do in the app on a working day (from three hours before work opens until it closes). If you forget **End my day**, the day ends at the last thing you did.
  - For field people, the phone shares your place for **Where the team is** only between Start my day and End my day, and only while the app is open.
  - Note: on a narrow phone screen this button is not shown in the top bar, so a phone user cannot press Start my day or End my day (the day still starts and ends by itself).
- **Screen**: `/`, login `hos1`, top bar ("In since").

### Finding your way: the menu, the phone bar and search
- **Who**: everyone.
- **When**: any time.
- **Steps**:
  1. The menu runs down the left. Your own work is at the top; a lead's or head's menu also has **Team** and **Look up** headings; **Me** and **Messages** sit at the foot (the CEO's and the Admin's Messages sit at the top).
  2. A link with **›** is a folder: press it to open the pages inside. A number beside a link or folder is what waits there.
  3. Sellers and leads have **Create New Order** on top of the menu.
  4. To hide or show the menu on a computer, press **☰** at the top left. On a phone, **☰** opens the menu, and a bar at the bottom holds the main links with **New order** raised in the middle.
  5. To find something, type at least two letters in **Search medicines, customers, orders, people** in the top bar. Pick a line from the list, or press Enter for the first one.
  6. To go back, press the small **‹** above the page title; the words beside it say where you are.
- **What happens next**: search opens the order, person, medicine or customer you picked. You only find the customers you are allowed to see.
- **Good to know**: an order code or a customer's PO number finds the order first.
- **Screen**: `/`, login `tl2`, the whole top bar and the menu down to "Me".

### Reading what needs you and the news
- **Who**: everyone.
- **When**: several times a day, when **Messages** shows a number.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Messages** in the menu.
  2. **To decide**: every decision waiting on you, from every desk, with how many and since when. Press a line to open the page where you decide. Below it: **What you asked for, still open**.
  3. **Needs you**: what waits on you (an order sent back, a leave request, a team's stopped order, an announcement). Press a line to open its page; it is then marked read.
  4. **Updates**: the news of what moved on your orders and your team.
  5. Press **Mark all read** to clear the count.
- **What happens next**: the numbers beside Messages go down as you read.
- **Good to know**: there is no separate bell; everything comes here.
- **Screen**: `/messages?box=important`, login `hos1`, top ("Needs you"); `/decisions`, login `tl2`, "Waiting on you".

### Sending a message or an announcement
- **Who**: everyone. Team leads may announce to the teams they lead; the CEO and the Admin may write to and announce to everyone.
- **When**: instead of an email between departments.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Messages › Chats** and press **New**.
  2. Pick who it goes to: **One person**, **A team**, **A few people**, or (CEO and Admin only) **Everyone**.
  3. Pick the person or the team from the list, or tick the people (**Find people…** helps).
  4. For a team you lead (or everyone), tick **Announcement** if they should only read it, not reply.
  5. Write a **Subject** (asked for a few people or an announcement) and the **Message**.
  6. Press **Send** (or **Announce**).
- **What happens next**: it reaches them in Messages. On your announcement you see **Seen by … of …**; press it to see who has read it.
- **Good to know**: to answer a chat, open it, type in **Write a message…** and press the send arrow. Use **All / Announcements / Teams / People** and **Find a conversation** to find an old one.
- **Screen**: `/messages`, login `hos1`, press "New" to show the "New message" window.

### Asking for leave
- **Who**: everyone except the CEO and the Admin (HR records theirs).
- **When**: before the days you will be away, or when you were sick.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Me › Leave & forms** (it is called **My leave & forms** when your menu also holds your team's Leave & forms).
  2. Check the cards: **Vacation leave left**, **Sick leave left**, **Leave without pay**.
  3. Press **Ask for leave**.
  4. Choose the **Kind**: Vacation, Sick, Without pay or Other (the days left show beside each).
  5. Set **From** and **To**, or tick **Half a day**.
  6. Write the **Reason** (needed for Other). For sick leave add the **Medical certificate (if you have one)** with **Take or pick a photo**.
  7. Press **Ask for leave**.
- **What happens next**: it goes to the person you report to (HR if you report to the CEO, the Admin or nobody); the page header says who. You see it as **Waiting**, then **Approved** or **Not approved** with their word. You are told under Messages.
- **Good to know**:
  - Rest days and holidays don't count. Regular staff have 10 vacation and 10 sick days a year; probationary staff, part-timers and interns ask for leave without pay.
  - A request past the days left, or overlapping other leave, is refused.
  - **Cancel it** works while it waits, or on approved leave that hasn't started.
- **Screen**: `/me/leave`, login `hos1`, top ("My leave & forms"); press "Ask for leave" for the window.

### Filing a form: official business, work from home, overtime
- **Who**: everyone except the CEO and the Admin.
- **When**: you worked away from the office, worked from home, or stayed after hours.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Me › Leave & forms** and press **File a form**.
  2. Pick the **Form**: **Official business**, **Work from home** or **Overtime**.
  3. Set the **Day**. For official business and overtime also set **From** and **To**; for official business say **Where**.
  4. Write **What it was for** and press **File the form**.
- **What happens next**: it goes to the same person as your leave. Once approved, official business counts as worked (not late or absent), work from home counts as a worked day, overtime counts as approved overtime.
- **Good to know**: one form of a kind a day. Your forms are listed under **Forms: official business, work from home, overtime**.
- **Screen**: `/me/leave`, login `hos1`, scroll to "Forms: official business, work from home, overtime".

### Reading your payslip
- **Who**: everyone paid through the company (not the CEO or the Admin, who have no Me folder).
- **When**: after HR releases a month.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Me › My payslips**.
  2. On the left, each released month shows **Paid** and the day, with the net pay. Press a month.
  3. Read the payslip: the company, your name and employee ID, the month and **Pay day**; the line about your attendance; **Earnings** and **Gross pay**; **Deductions** and **Total deductions**; **Taxable pay** and **Net pay**.
  4. Press **Print** to print it.
- **What happens next**: nothing to do; a released payslip doesn't change (a later correction goes on the next month).
- **Good to know**: a month HR hasn't released isn't shown ("None released yet"). The pay month runs from the 1st to the month's last day.
- **Screen**: `/me/payslips`, login `hos1`, top ("My payslips").

### Changing your password (only if you want to)
- **Who**: anyone. Nobody has to change the company's common password.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press your initials at the top right, then **Change password**.
  2. Type your **Current password** and the **New password (8 characters or more)**, then save.
- **Good to know**: if the Admin later gives you a new password, your login goes back to the company's common password.
- **Screen**: `/password`, login `hos1`, top ("Change password").

### Signing out
- **Who**: everyone, always on a shared computer.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press your initials at the top right. Your name and job show at the top of the small menu.
  2. Press **Sign out**.
- **What happens next**: the company portal opens at once, ready for the next person.
- **Good to know**: on a wide screen the round button with your workspace's name (for example **Sales and field**) also signs you out and takes you back to the portal to choose another workspace.
- **Screen**: `/`, login `hos1`, top right, press the initials to open the menu.

### Reporting a complaint or side-effect
- **Who**: anyone a customer told about a problem with a medicine, a delivery or the service.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Messages › Complaints & side-effects** and press **Report one**.
  2. Choose what it is **About** (Product quality, Side-effect, Wrong or damaged delivery, Service, Other). For a side-effect, tick **Serious** if the patient was hospitalised, in danger, disabled or died.
  3. Pick the **Medicine**, type the **Batch number**, the **Patient (if any)**, **We heard of it on** and **What happened**.
  4. Press **Report it**.
- **What happens next**: Regulatory handles it to its due day (a serious side-effect within 15 days). You see the ones you reported.
- **Screen**: `/complaints`, login `hos1`, top ("Complaints and side-effects").

## Part 2. Team leads and heads

Team leads (District Sales Coach, Sales Coach, B2B Sales Coach…) and heads (Business Mentor, PH Country Mentor, Sales and Marketing Mentor…) sign in at **Sales and field**. Each seat's menu is a little different; the pages are the same.

### Reading your Home
- **Who**: team leads and heads.
- **When**: first thing in the morning, and through the day.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Home**. The greeting shows your job and "your district" or your team.
  2. Use the month box at the top right (**‹ November 2026 ›**) to look at a past month.
  3. Read the four boxes: **Sold this month** (what Finance invoiced, with the bar against the target and "pace"), **Today**, **Orders placed** (placed is not yet sold: "sold when Finance invoices") and **Stopped now** (on hold or sent back).
  4. A Hospital District Sales Coach then sees **Needs you today** (who went three working days without an invoice, collections over 90 days, no huddle), **District now** (each CHC worst first: sales vs target, days to collect, lapsed, zero-sales days, tonight's huddle), **Tonight's huddle** and **15th rescue**.
  5. Other leads see the team at a glance: **Who sold most in the team**, **Orders right now**, **Medicines sold**, **Customers**, how long each desk takes, then **Your people**: each person against their target, with orders placed, waiting and stopped.
  6. **Stopped now** at the foot lists each stopped order; press one to open it.
- **What happens next**: chase what is red; press a person to open their score.
- **Good to know**: a head's Home adds their division's view (each team against target, people at risk, money owed).
- **Screen**: `/`, login `tl2`, top ("Sold this month") and scroll to "District now".

### Switching between your teams
- **Who**: a lead or head with more than one team under them (for example a coach who leads two teams).
- **When**: to look at one team at a time, or all together.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **My team** in the menu. Under **Whole team** your teams are listed, each with its number of people. Press a team.
  2. Or, on a team page, use the row of buttons at the top: **All · my 2 teams** for both together, or one team (its name and its lead). A team with teams of its own opens a second row.
- **What happens next**: the whole page turns to that team, and stays on it as you move between pages that have the row.
- **Good to know**: the row is on **My team**, **Scorecards**, **Targets**, **Sales performance**, **Collections**, **Team orders**, **Calls**, the **Visits report** and **Who's working**. It shows only when you have two or more teams.
- **Screen**: `/my-team`, login `tl14`, top ("All · my 2 teams").

### Following your team's orders
- **Who**: team leads and heads.
- **When**: daily, and whenever **Stopped now** shows a number.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Team orders** (a head's is **Division orders** or **Business orders**).
  2. Pick a tab: **All**, **Sent back**, **With Management**, **With Finance**, **Packing or on the way**, **On hold**, **Delivered this month**. Each shows its count.
  3. Orders are grouped by the day placed; each card shows the seller. Press a card to open the order and see where it stands.
  4. **Returns** at the end of the tabs opens the returns.
- **What happens next**: you chase the person or the desk. Only the seller puts a sent-back order right; you can put right only an order in your own name.
- **Screen**: `/orders`, login `tl2`, top ("Team orders").

### Placing an order for one of your people
- **Who**: team leads and heads.
- **When**: a customer orders through you, or a MedRep can't place it themselves.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Create New Order** at the top of the menu.
  2. **1 General**: check the **Division**.
  3. **2 Customer details**: find and pick the customer (or **New customer**).
  4. In **Whose order is it?** pick the MedRep. The customer's own MedRep is chosen already ("this customer's MedRep"). **Mine (no MedRep: an empty seat's customer)** keeps it in your name.
  5. Fill the rest of **Customer details**, then **Medicines**, **Documents** (prescription, PO, proof of payment), and **Review and place**.
  6. Press **Place order**, check the **Confirm** window and press **Confirm and place**.
- **What happens next**: the order counts for that MedRep's sales and target, and they follow it like their own. It goes to Management.
- **Good to know**: the Division follows the person whose order it is ("their own team lead and head").
- **Screen**: `/orders/new`, login `tl2`, pick a customer, scroll to "Whose order is it?".

### Approving expenses
- **Who**: the team lead the person reports to (field teams).
- **When**: when **Expenses to approve** has lines.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Team visits › Expenses to approve** (a head: **Visits** or **Field visits › Expenses to approve**).
  2. Each line shows the kind (Petrol, Fare, Parking…), the amount, kilometres for petrol, the person, the day and the visit.
  3. Press **Bill** to see the photo of the bill.
  4. Press **Approve**, or **Send back**, write **What is wrong** and press **Send back**.
- **What happens next**: an approved line is fixed. A sent-back line goes to the person, who presses **Put it right** and sends it again.
- **Good to know**: you can't approve your own. Note: an approved expense does not go anywhere for payment yet (not to the payslip, not to Finance).
- **Screen**: `/visits/expenses?waiting=1`, login `tl2`, top ("Expenses to approve").

### Approving a visit plan
- **Who**: field team leads.
- **When**: at the start of the month, when a MedRep sends their plan.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Team visits › Visits report**. A yellow bar shows **Plans waiting for you:** with names.
  2. Press a name. Their month's plan opens.
  3. Press **Approve**, or **Send back** with a word.
- **What happens next**: the MedRep sees Approved or Sent back.
- **Screen**: `/field/report`, login `tl2`, top ("Plans waiting for you").

### The evening huddle
- **Who**: Hospital Division coaches and the Business Mentor (**Team huddle** in their menu).
- **When**: after 7 PM, when the CHCs have declared their day.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Team huddle**. The top line says how many declared and how many had no invoice.
  2. Read each person's **Sales**, **Collected**, **Visits**, **Invoice** (Yes/No) and **Blocker · tomorrow**.
  3. A row marked **3 days: go with them tomorrow** has had no invoice three working days running.
  4. Use **‹ ›** to see another day.
- **What happens next**: nothing to press; you follow up with the person. The same summary is on your Home as **Tonight's huddle**.
- **Good to know**: before anyone declares, the numbers come from the day's invoices, payments and visits ("not declared").
- **Screen**: `/huddle`, login `tl2`, top ("Team huddle").

### Reading scorecards and answering a question on the figures
- **Who**: team leads and heads.
- **When**: through the month and at its end.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **My team › Scorecards** (a head: **Scorecards**).
  2. Pick the month at the top right. Each row is a seat: **Score** out of its maximum (a blue dot means the month is still running), each KPI's points, **12-month** average and **Increment**. **Plan** marks an improvement plan.
  3. Press a name to open their whole card.
  4. Under **Questions on the figures**, read what a person asked. Type **Your answer** and press **Upheld: fix the source** or **Turn down**.
- **What happens next**: the person sees your answer on their card.
- **Screen**: `/team-scores`, login `tl2`, top ("Scorecards").

### Setting targets
- **Who**: team leads and heads for the people under them; the CEO and the Admin for anyone.
- **When**: before the month starts, or on its first days.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Targets**. Use **‹ ›** at the top to pick the month.
  2. To start from last month, press **Use last month's for the empty ones**.
  3. On a person's card, type the amount in **Target for the month (₱)** and press **Save** (or Enter). **Same as last month: …** fills it in one click.
  4. Optionally fill **TLB (SKUs)** and **Billing (customers)** and press **Save counts** (the peso target first).
  5. Under **Empty seats**, type a target for a seat nobody holds yet.
- **What happens next**: each card shows **On pace**, **Behind** or **Far behind**, and what is left a day. A target counts what Finance invoiced in the month.
- **Good to know**: an empty seat's target goes to whoever takes the seat.
- **Screen**: `/targets`, login `tl2`, scroll to "Target for the month (₱)".

### Team attendance: who's working
- **Who**: team leads and heads.
- **When**: in the morning and any time.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Team attendance › Who's working** (a head: **Attendance › Who's working**).
  2. The boxes count **Working now**, **Done for the day**, **Not in yet**, **Came late**, **On leave**. Press one to show only those people.
  3. Use **‹**, the date and **›** to see another day. **Find a person or team** searches.
  4. Press a name to open their month: each day's **In**, **Out**, **Hours**, **Late**, **Undertime**, **After hours** and **Note**.
- **What happens next**: only HR puts a day right; tell HR if a day is wrong.
- **Screen**: `/hr/attendance`, login `tl2`, top ("Who's working").

### Approving leave and forms for your team
- **Who**: the person each one reports to (HR may decide any).
- **When**: when **Leave & forms** shows a number.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Team attendance › Leave & forms**.
  2. Pick **Leave** or **Forms** at the top. The tab **Waiting for you** is open.
  3. Read the line (name, kind, days, reason, any paper). Press the name to see their month.
  4. Press **Approve**, or **Not approved**, write **Why not** and press **Not approved**.
- **What happens next**: the person is told under Messages. Approved leave shows on their attendance.
- **Good to know**: the other tabs are **Asked**, **Approved**, **Not approved**, **Cancelled**; **‹ ›** changes the month, **Every month** shows all.
- **Screen**: `/hr/leave`, login `tl2`, top ("Waiting for you").

### Where the team is
- **Who**: field team leads and heads.
- **When**: during the working day.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Team visits › Where the team is** (a head: **Visits › Where the team is**).
  2. The top line says how many share a place now and how many are working. Each person shows **Live**, **Working · no place** or **Not working**, and the visit they are at.
  3. Press **Open on the map** to see their place on a map.
- **What happens next**: nothing to change; it is for looking.
- **Good to know**: a place shows only while the person's day runs (Start my day to End my day) and their app is open; a place older than half an hour isn't shown as where they are.
- **Screen**: `/field/live`, login `tl2`, top ("Where the team is").

### Asking for a new person, region or area
- **Who**: team leads and heads.
- **When**: your team needs another seat, a new region or a new area.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **My team**. At the top press **Ask for**: **Person**, **Region** or **Area**.
  2. Fill the window (the team, the job, its area and cities; a name and phone if you have someone; why) and press **Send to the CEO**.
- **What happens next**: "Sent to the CEO". The CEO or the Admin approves or declines it under **Asks for people**; your team is told.
- **Screen**: `/my-team`, login `tl2`, top ("Ask for").

## Part 3. HR

HR signs in at the **HR** tile. The menu: **People** (Home), **Joining**, **Who's working**, **Leave & forms**, **Payroll**, **Attendance report**, **Medicines & stock**; at the foot **Me** (**My leave & forms**, **My payslips**) and **Messages**.

### Finding a person's record
- **Who**: HR (the CEO reads).
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **People**. The tabs: **Working**, **Joining** (first day not come yet), **Details missing**, **Left**.
  2. Type in **Find a name, ID or team**.
  3. The list shows **Person**, **Company**, **Started**, **Signs in with** and **Record** (**Complete**, or what is missing).
  4. Press a name to open the record: job, **Login** (**Signs in with**, **Last signed in**), **Contact**, **Pay and IDs**, **Seats held**.
- **Screen**: `/`, login `hr1`, top ("People").

### Adding a new joiner
- **Who**: HR (the Admin as backup).
- **When**: before or on the person's first day.
- **Steps**:
  1. Make sure the seat exists: the joiner goes into a vacant seat. If the list says "No vacant seat: the Admin makes one first", ask the Admin.
  2. On **People**, press **New joiner**.
  3. **Who**: **Full name**, **Mobile**.
  4. **Job**: pick the **Seat** (vacant seats, by team), **First day**, **Company**, **Employed as**, **Employee ID**, **Position**.
  5. **Pay (monthly, seen by HR and the CEO)**: **Salary (₱)**, **Allowance (₱)**.
  6. **Login you give them**: **Login email**. The password is the company's common one ("the same for everyone").
  7. Press **Add joiner**.
- **What happens next**: a window shows their login; press **Copy** and hand it to them yourself (the system sends nothing). **Open their record** opens it. A joiner whose first day hasn't come sits under **Joining**.
- **Good to know**: they can also sign in with the seat's login ID, from their first day. Note: the form insists on an email-style login, although people sign in with the seat's login ID.
- **Screen**: `/`, login `hr1`, press "New joiner" for the window.

### Keeping a record up to date, and someone leaving
- **Who**: HR.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the person from **People**.
  2. Press **Edit record** to change **Job** (company, employed as, hired on, regular since), **Contact**, **Pay and IDs** (salary, allowance, bank, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG…). Save.
  3. Press **Change login** to change the **Login email** or give a **New password** (leave it empty to keep theirs) and press **Save login**. A new password signs them out everywhere.
  4. When someone leaves, press **Mark as left**, set the **Last day** and **Why**, and press **Mark as left**.
- **What happens next**: after leaving, their login stops and their seat falls vacant; the record is kept and the seat's customers wait for the next person. **Back at work** undoes it.
- **Screen**: `/hr/people/529`, login `hr1`, top (the person's name and "Edit record").

### Seeing who's working today
- **Who**: HR (everyone), the CEO and the Admin.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Who's working**. The boxes: **Working now**, **Done for the day**, **Not in yet**, **Came late**, **On leave**; press one to filter.
  2. Change the day with **‹ ›** or the date. **Report** opens the month report.
  3. The table shows **Status**, **In**, **Out**, **Hours**, **Late** and **This month**.
- **Screen**: `/hr/attendance`, login `hr1`, top ("Who's working").

### Putting a day right
- **Who**: HR (the Admin as backup).
- **When**: someone forgot to start or end the day, or worked outside the office.
- **Steps**:
  1. In **Who's working**, press the person's name. Their month opens; change the month with **‹ ›**.
  2. On the day, press the pencil.
  3. Set **Time in** and **Time out** (as the day really was) and write **Why** (for example "Forgot to sign in; was at the hospital from 7:30").
  4. Press **Save the day**.
- **What happens next**: the day, the person's month and the draft payroll change at once; the note shows on the day.
- **Good to know**: a day in the future can't be set; time out must come after time in.
- **Screen**: `/hr/attendance/529`, login `hr1`, the day rows (pencil at the right).

### Recording leave for someone
- **Who**: HR.
- **When**: leave asked outside the app.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the person's month (from **Who's working**) and press **Record leave**.
  2. Choose **Kind**, **From**, **To** (or **Half a day**), write the **Reason**.
  3. Press **Record leave**.
- **What happens next**: it is approved as recorded and shows under **Leave this month**.
- **Screen**: `/hr/attendance/529`, login `hr1`, press "Record leave".

### Approving leave and forms
- **Who**: HR decides for people who report to the CEO, the Admin or nobody, and may decide anyone's.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Leave & forms**, pick **Leave** or **Forms**, tab **Waiting for you**.
  2. Press **Approve**, or **Not approved** with **Why not**.
- **What happens next**: the person is told under Messages.
- **Screen**: `/hr/leave`, login `hr1`, top ("Leave").

### Reading the attendance report
- **Who**: HR, the CEO, the Admin.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Attendance report** and pick the days.
  2. Sort by **Fewest hours**, **Most late**, **Most absent**, **Most leave** or **Name**.
  3. Read each person's **Hours worked**, **Days in**, **A day**, **Late**, **Undertime**, **Overtime**, **Absent**, **Leave**.
- **Screen**: `/hr/report`, login `hr1`, top ("Attendance report").

### Running the month's payroll
- **Who**: HR (the CEO reads the same pages and changes nothing).
- **When**: through the month, and at its end.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Payroll**. Each month shows **Pay day**, **Status** (**Running**, **Ready to release**, **Released**), **People**, **Gross pay**, **Net pay**. Press a month.
  2. Read the boxes: **People paid**, **Gross pay**, **Deductions**, **Net pay**, **Company's shares**. A yellow bar lists people "Not paid here until their salary is on their record": add their salary on their record.
  3. Press a person in the table to see their payslip on the right.
  4. To add a cash advance, loan, bonus or correction, press **Add a line**: **What it is**, **Amount (₱)**, **Takes off (loan, cash advance)** or **Adds (bonus, correction)**, a **Note (shown on the payslip)**, then **Add the line**.
  5. **Sheet** downloads the register.
- **What happens next**: until release, the payroll is "a draft from the DTR as it stands": a day put right shows at once.
- **Good to know**: **Rates** shows the law's rates (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, withholding tax) and the company's own rules (a day's pay is the monthly salary × 12 ÷ 313).
- **Screen**: `/hr/payroll/2026-11`, login `hr1`, top ("Payroll · November 2026").

### Releasing payslips
- **Who**: HR.
- **When**: once the month has ended.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the month in **Payroll**.
  2. Press **Release payslips**. The window says how many payslips, the net pay and the pay day.
  3. Press **Release payslips**.
- **What happens next**: each person is told and reads their own under **My payslips**. The payslips are frozen; a later change goes on the next month.
- **Good to know**: while the month runs, the button is not there and the page says from which day it can be released. Note: pay day is the month's last day but the month can only be released from the next day, so payslips can't be out on pay day itself.
- **Screen**: `/hr/payroll/2026-10`, login `hr1`, top ("Release payslips" or "Released").

## Part 5. The Admin

The Admin signs in at the **Administration** tile. The **Admin panel** menu: **Channels** (Home), **Teams & people**, **Areas**, **Asks for people**, **Who approves what**, **Targets**, **Old customers to seats**, **Company chart (as built)**, **Messages**; under **Company**: **Company details**, **Online pages & mail**, **Data checks**, **Audit**, **Calls**. The CEO can do everything here too.

### Adding a channel and setting its Division
- **Who**: the Admin (or the CEO).
- **Steps**:
  1. On **Channels**, press **Add channel** (or the **A new channel** card).
  2. Type the **Code** (for example HOSP) and the **Name**, and pick the **Division on its orders**.
  3. Press **Add channel**.
- **What happens next**: the channel's page opens; its box at the top of the company is made with it. Orders, sales, targets and the CEO's figures take it at once.
- **Good to know**: to change it later, open the channel and press **Name & Division** (the code can't change). **Take out of use** retires a channel; **Show channels out of use** and **Bring back into use** return it.
- **Screen**: `/`, login `admin`, top ("Channels").

### Building a channel: head, teams, team leads, MedReps
- **Who**: the Admin.
- **Steps**:
  1. On **Channels**, press the channel's card.
  2. **Head of the channel**: press **Add the head**.
  3. **Teams, their team leads and MedReps**: press **Add team** (**Add the first team**), then on a team **Add its team lead** and **+ MedRep**. A team's **…** also has **A vacant MedRep seat (fill later)**, **Add a team under it**, **Move under…** and **Rename**.
  4. **Areas**: press **Add area**.
- **What happens next**: each person added gets a seat and a login (see the next procedure).
- **Screen**: `/admin/channels/1`, login `admin`, scroll to "Head of the channel".

### Giving a person a seat and a login
- **Who**: the Admin (or the CEO).
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Teams & people** and the **Teams** tab. Pick the team in the tree on the left (on a phone, from the **Team** list).
  2. Press **Add person** (or **Add MedRep** beside a kind of work), or **Fill** on a **Vacant** seat.
  3. **Who**: **Name**, **Employee ID**, **Phone**, **Their own email (optional)** (to reach them, not their login).
  4. For a new seat, **Their job**: **Work they do**, **Job title**, **Leads …** (tick for the team's lead), **Reports to**, **Area (their customers)** (or **+ A new area…** with its cities).
  5. Set the **First day** and press **Add and make their login**.
- **What happens next**: the window "… can sign in" shows the **Login ID** (the seat's) and says everyone signs in with the company's common password. Press **Copy login**, hand it over, press **Done**. The login ID also shows on the seat's row.
- **Good to know**: on the Chart tab, **+** on a person's box adds someone under them. The first day can't be in the future here; for a joiner who starts later, HR's **New joiner** takes a future first day.
- **Screen**: `/people?tab=teams`, login `admin`, the team panel ("Add person").

### Moving someone
- **Who**: the Admin (or the CEO).
- **Steps** (pick one way):
  1. **To another team, same seat**: on the **Teams** tab, drag the person's row onto a team in the tree. A green bar asks "Move … into …?". Press **Move**.
  2. **Change their job, boss, area or team**: on the row press **…** › **Change the seat (work, boss, area)** (on the Chart, the pencil). Change **Team**, **Work they do**, **Reports to** or **Area**, then **Save the seat**.
  3. **Into a different seat**: on the vacant seat press **Fill** › **Someone already here**, pick the **Person**, set **From**. Leave **Keep their seat too (two jobs)** unticked to free their old seat. Press **Put them in the seat**.
- **What happens next**: a seat keeps its customers and orders; whoever sits in it follows the seat's work, boss and area.
- **Good to know**: dragging a team moves it with all its seats and people.
- **Screen**: `/people?tab=teams`, login `admin`, the tree's foot ("Drag a person's seat…").

### A vacant seat: making it, covering it, filling it
- **Who**: the Admin (or the CEO).
- **Steps**:
  1. To make one: in the team panel press **…** › **A vacant seat (fill later)**, set the job and press **Add the seat**.
  2. Someone covers it meanwhile: on the seat press **…** › **Someone covers it meanwhile** (or **Someone covers (leave)** while its person is away). Pick **Who covers**, the **Last day** and **Why**, press **Start the cover**. **End …'s cover now** stops it early.
  3. To fill it: press **Fill** (see "Giving a person a seat and a login").
  4. To remove a seat nobody needs: **…** › **Close this seat**.
- **What happens next**: a vacant seat's customers wait with the team's lead; while covered, the people under it report to whoever covers. A cover ends by itself after its last day.
- **Good to know**: the top of **Teams & people** counts **Vacant seats**, **Covering a seat** and **Signed in, no seat**.
- **Screen**: `/people?tab=teams`, login `admin`, a row marked "Vacant".

### Someone is no longer with the company
- **Who**: the Admin (HR uses **Mark as left** on the record; either is enough).
- **Steps**:
  1. On the person's row press **…** › **No longer with us…**.
  2. Read what becomes vacant, pick **Why** and press **… has left**.
- **What happens next**: their login stops now; their seat becomes vacant and its customers and open orders stay with it for whoever fills it.
- **Screen**: `/people?tab=teams`, login `admin`, a seat row's "…" menu.

### Giving a new password
- **Who**: the Admin (or the CEO).
- **When**: someone forgot their password or is locked out.
- **Steps**:
  1. On the person's row press **…** › **Give a new password** (on the Chart the same thing is called **Show their login**).
  2. The window shows their **Login ID** and password; press **Copy login** and hand it over.
- **What happens next**: their password goes back to the company's common password, and they are signed out on every device. (If no common password is set on the server, a new one is shown once and they choose their own at the next sign-in.)
- **Good to know**: Note: **Show their login** on the Chart does not just show the login; it resets the password and signs the person out everywhere. Use it only when that is what you want.
- **Screen**: `/people`, login `admin`, a box's "…" menu.

### Areas
- **Who**: the Admin (or the CEO).
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Areas**. Each channel lists its areas with their cities, the seat holding each (or "No seat holds it") and their customers.
  2. Press **Add area** on a channel. Keep the name's form (for example "HOS | Pasay"), type the **Cities in it (comma between)** and press **Add area**. Press an area's pencil to change it.
  3. To give an area to a seat, change the seat (**Change the seat** › **Area**).
- **What happens next**: customers in its cities who have no area yet join it; the MedRep in the seat holding the area gets them.
- **Screen**: `/people?tab=areas`, login `admin`, top ("Add area").

### Asks for people
- **Who**: the CEO or the Admin.
- **When**: when **Asks for people** shows a number (team leads ask from My team).
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Asks for people**. Each ask shows **Waiting for you**.
  2. Press **Approve** (with **A note (optional)**) or **Decline** (with **Why**), then **Approve** / **Decline**.
- **What happens next**: an approved ask makes the seat, region or area; the team is told either way.
- **Screen**: `/people?tab=requests`, login `admin`, top.

### Who approves what
- **Who**: the CEO or the Admin.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Who approves what**. Each Management person shows what they approve now.
  2. Choose **Every division**, or **Only these** and tick the divisions.
  3. Press **Save**.
- **What happens next**: their piles hold only those orders, and another division's order can't be approved by them. An order with no division is for those who approve everything.
- **Screen**: `/people?tab=approvers`, login `admin`, top.

### Targets for the whole company
- **Who**: the Admin and the CEO (anyone's); leads for their own people.
- **Steps**: as in "Setting targets" (Part 2): **Targets**, the month, **Use last month's for the empty ones**, each person's **Target for the month (₱)**, and **Empty seats**.
- **Screen**: `/targets`, login `admin`, scroll to "Empty seats".

### Data checks
- **Who**: the Admin.
- **When**: daily, and after a big change or an import.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Data checks**. The top line says "Everything adds up." or how many checks need a look.
  2. Each check shows **Fine** or how many are wrong, with the orders, bills, medicines or people concerned.
  3. Press **Check again** after something is put right.
- **What happens next**: tell the desk concerned (Finance, Inventory…) what to put right.
- **Screen**: `/admin/data-checks`, login `admin`, top ("Data checks").

### Audit
- **Who**: the Admin and the CEO.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Audit**.
  2. Filter by kind (**Everything** or one kind), **Who (a name)**, **Words, an order or invoice number**, and the dates.
  3. Read **When**, who, **Record** and **What happened**.
- **Screen**: `/audit`, login `admin`, top ("Audit").

### Company details and online pages
- **Who**: the Admin (or the CEO).
- **Steps**:
  1. **Company details**: fill **Company name**, **TIN**, **Address**, **Phone**, **Email**, **FDA licence (LTO)**, **Line at the foot of every page**, **Signs the quotations**, **Their title**, tick **VAT-registered**, press **Save**. These print on quotations.
  2. **Online pages & mail** (the page is titled "Channels"): the Facebook pages and mailboxes the Online desk reads, and how their reading goes.
- **Screen**: `/settings`, login `admin`, top ("Company details").
