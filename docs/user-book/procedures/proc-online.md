# The Online desk (and the PAP case)

The Online desk answers people who write on Messenger, through the websites or by email, or who
call, and turns each enquiry into an order. In the company chart the seat is the **B2C CHC**
(logins `online1`, Mary Jane Viñas, and `online2`, Rachelle Upao). The **PAP officer** (login
`pap1`, Fhaye Norella) uses the same case page for patient-assistance (guarantee letter) cases.

**Every enquiry is a case.** A case page has the same parts every time:

- **The top:** the name, the case number (ENQ-…), *Enquiry* or *Website order*, where it came
  in, the step it is at, **Case details**, the **Owner**, phone, email and address, and one line
  with the next thing to do (green arrow). On the right, **how they pay** (Regular,
  Senior / PWD, GL).
- **Case journey:** a chart of the case's road. On its right sits the **Messenger** or
  **Email** button when the case has a conversation.
- **Orders on 09xx…:** this customer's earlier orders and enquiries, the **Customer page**
  link, and their **Points**.
- **Steps** (left) with the open step's work on the right:
  1 **Enquiry & quotation** (on a guarantee letter also 1.1 **Agency application** and
  1.2 **Guarantee letter**), 2 **Rx & documents**, 3 **Order & delivery**,
  4 **Payment & follow-up** (on a guarantee letter: **Claim & payment**). Under the steps:
  **Case actions**, **Money** and **Activity** (with **Add a note…**).
- The open step is in the address: `?step=quote`, `apply`, `letter`, `docs`, `order`, `pay`.
  A case opens on the step where its next action is.

Any Online desk agent may work any Online case; the **Owner** says whose it is. Management and
the CEO can open cases but only read them.

### 1. Start the day on Home

- **Who:** Online desk agent (B2C CHC).
- **When:** 8:00 every working day, and again during the day (Home refreshes itself every minute).
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign in. You land on **Home** ("Good morning, …", "Online desk · every new enquiry answered
     within 15 minutes").
  2. Read the six **morning pulse** tiles. A green tick is fine; red needs action. Each tile says
     what to do; click it to open the list behind it.
     - **Enquiries waiting for a reply** → Leads & enquiries.
     - **New prospects not answered** (people who are not customers yet) → Leads & enquiries.
     - **Refill-due list** → Refills due.
     - **An invoice on** *(last working day)*: Yes / No. "No" means a zero-sales day: call a
       lapsed customer before noon.
     - **Cancellations without a reason** → My orders.
     - **Replies past 15 minutes today**.
  3. Work **Waiting for a reply**, oldest first. Each row shows the name, **New prospect** or
     **Nobody's yet** (no owner), the source, the last message and how long they have waited
     (red once past 15 minutes). Click **Reply**: the case opens.
  4. Then work **Refills due** (due now and in the next two days): **Call** dials the number;
     **New order** opens the order form for that patient.
  5. Look at **My orders now**: *Quoted, not ordered*, *With Management or Finance*,
     *Packing or on the way*, *Day-3 check-ins due* (see procedure 20).
- **What happens next:** Each Reply takes you to the case; answering there or in the Inbox
  takes the row off the list.
- **Good to know:**
  - The playbook order: overnight enquiries answered by 8:30, new prospects first, the refill
    list before 12:00 noon, at least one invoice by 2:30 PM.
  - A reply is late when the customer's first message waits more than 15 minutes in working
    hours. Three late in one day shows red on your coach's **Online desk now** card.
  - **Waiting for a reply** lists your own cases and cases with no owner. Home shows at most
    10 refills; the full list is in **Refills due**.
- **Screen:** `/` as `online1`. (On the local copy: Refill-due list 1, invoice "No", nothing
  waiting.)

### 2. Find a case on Leads & enquiries

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** Any time you look for a case or work through them.
- **Steps:**
  1. Menu **Leads & enquiries**. The number beside the title is the open (active) cases.
  2. Pick a queue: **All**, **Active** (the default), **Waiting on you**, **To deliver**,
     **Payment**, **Completed**, **Lost**.
  3. Narrow by step with the step line: **New → Rx → Quote → Papers / GL → Ready → Ordered →
     Delivered, unpaid → Done**, and **Lost** apart. Click a step again to clear it.
  4. Search ("Search name, phone, medicine or city…") or use the filters: **All types /
     Enquiries / Website orders**, **All sources**, **Every road**, **Everyone's / Mine**.
     **Clear filters** resets them.
  5. On a wide screen choose the look: **Cards**, **Table** or **Board**.
  6. Click a card to open the case.
- **What happens next:** The case opens on its current step.
- **Good to know:**
  - A card shows: name ("for …" when someone writes for a patient), phone, **Needs reply**,
    **New** or **Completed**, the time it came in, **B2B · drugstore** for a business, *Enquiry*
    or *Website order*, the source, the payment road, the city, **Papers 1/2**, the medicine,
    the owner, the order number and its status, the **Next action**, and the step bar.
  - A blue-tinted card has not been opened yet. A green card is complete; a pink card is lost.
  - **Waiting on you** = a message not answered, or an order on hold or sent back.
  - Cards are grouped by the day they came in; **Show … more** loads more than 300.
- **Screen:** `/leads` as `online1`.

### 3. Answer a conversation in the Inbox and open its case

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** The **Inbox** count in the menu shows conversations waiting for a reply.
- **Steps:**
  1. Menu **Inbox**. Chats are on the left: those waiting for us first (blue **Needs reply**;
     bold = unread), then the newest.
  2. Filter with **All**, **Needs reply**, **Mine**, or **Search**. With more than one page or
     mailbox, pick it in **All channels**.
  3. Click a chat. The conversation opens in the middle: theirs in grey on the left, ours in blue
     on the right, the team's notes in amber in the middle, photos in line, "Seen" under the last
     message of ours they read.
  4. To answer, keep **Reply on Messenger** (or **Reply by email**) selected, type, and press
     Enter (Shift+Enter for a new line) or the send button.
  5. To leave a word for colleagues only, click **Note for the team**, type and send.
  6. On the right (wide screens) you see who they are: step, road, the next action, phone,
     email, address, what they asked for, who works it, and "N photos from the chat to file on
     the case". Click **Open the case** (or **Open case** above the conversation).
- **What happens next:** Once you answer, the chat leaves *Needs reply* and the Home list.
  Photos they sent wait on the case under **Rx & documents → From the chat, not filed yet**
  (procedure 12).
- **Good to know:**
  - **The same chat is inside the case:** on the case, click **Messenger** or **Email** on the
    Case journey bar (a red dot means they are waiting). It opens on the right; **Close chat**,
    the X or Esc closes it.
  - A person's first Messenger message or email opens a new case by itself, named from their
    profile ("Facebook user" when unknown); later messages land on their newest case. These
    cases have **no owner**: set yourself as Owner (procedure 7) and type their real name in
    Case details.
  - Messenger takes a reply only within 24 hours of their last message. After that the box
    says "More than 24 hours since their last message: Messenger won't deliver a reply. Call or
    text them."
  - A case closed as lost can't be answered ("Closed as lost: reopen the case to answer. A note
    for the team still goes."). If they write again, the case opens again by itself.
  - **Not built:** joining a chat to another existing case, or merging two cases. The chat's
    case finds the patient on file by **phone number**: add the number in Case details. Also not
    built: sending our own photos or files in the chat, saved replies, an automatic reply.
  - No page or mailbox connected: "No Facebook page or mailbox is connected yet. Ask the Admin
    to connect one."
- **Screen:** `/inbox` as `online1`; one chat: `/inbox?chat=<case number>`. (The local copy has
  no page or mailbox connected, so it shows the "No Facebook page or mailbox…" line.)

### 5. Add a lead by hand (New lead)

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** A call, a walk-in, a referral, or any enquiry that did not come through a connected
  page, mailbox or website.
- **Steps:**
  1. Optional but wise: search **Patients** (menu) for their number first.
  2. Click **New lead** at the top (or on Leads & enquiries). The **New enquiry** window opens.
  3. Fill in:
     - **Who is writing or calling** (required) and **They are**: The patient themself, Spouse,
       Parent, Son / daughter, Sibling, Caregiver, Other representative.
     - When it is not the patient: **The patient's name** (required).
     - **Phone**. If the number is already a customer, a blue line says "Already a customer on
       this number: … The order will go to them."
     - **Email**, or tick **No email**.
     - **Delivery address** and **City / province**.
     - **Came in through**: Messenger, Website, Facebook, Instagram, 2MG website, Caps & Pills
       website, Call, Walk-in, Referral, Email, Other.
     - **Owner**: you by default.
     - **What they are asking for**: medicine, strength, how many.
  4. Under **How will they pay?** pick **Regular** (full price), **Senior citizen / PWD** (20%
     off, no VAT) or **Guarantee letter** (agency pays). For a guarantee letter also pick
     **Which agency gives the letter?**: DSWD (AICS), PCSO (IMAP), OVP (Office of the Vice
     President), OP (Office of the President (PMS)).
  5. Click **Add enquiry**.
- **What happens next:** The case opens with its ENQ number, on step 1 **Enquiry & quotation**.
  Activity says "Enquiry from … added by …".
- **Good to know:**
  - The email is asked every time: "Type their email, or tick “No email”: every step of the
    order is emailed to them."
  - The phone number is the customer: its last ten digits find the patient on file.
  - A guarantee letter needs the agency ("Pick the agency that gives the letter.").
- **Screen:** `/leads?new=1` as `online1`.

### 6. Choose how they pay (the payment road)

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** At the start of a case, or when you learn the patient is a senior / PWD or has a
  guarantee letter. Before the order is placed.
- **Steps:**
  1. At the top right of the case, click **Regular**, **Senior / PWD** or **GL**. The line
     under the buttons says the road ("Regular — no discount", "Senior citizen or PWD — 20% off,
     no VAT", "GL — PCSO").
  2. On a GL case, pick the agency in **Choose agency**.
  3. To switch a case **to** GL, use **Case details** → **How will they pay?** →
     **Guarantee letter** and pick the agency there, then **Save and start** (see Good to know).
- **What each road needs:**

  | Road | Price | Papers before the order | How it is paid |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Regular** | The patient price | **Prescription** (stops the order until filed); **Patient's valid ID** (asked, checked by Management, does not stop the order) | Cash on delivery or paid in advance; points can be used |
  | **Senior / PWD** | VAT taken out of a 12%-VAT medicine, then 20% off (two lines on the quotation) | **Prescription** and one **Senior citizen or PWD ID** (either card) | Cash on delivery or paid in advance; no points |
  | **Guarantee letter** | The quotation is for the agency | **Prescription**, **Patient's valid ID**, **Medical certificate or clinical abstract**, and the **Guarantee letter** (photo, number, amount, verified) | Billed to the agency, 60 days; no points |
  | **A business buying** (procedure 8) | The business's own price list | None | Regular road only: cash on delivery, in advance, or its own terms |

- **What happens next:** The case's papers, next line and step change to the new road. A
  quotation saved for another road shows "Saved for another road or buyer. Check the prices and
  save it again for this one."
- **Good to know:**
  - The senior / PWD relief is the law, so Management is not asked about it.
  - Once the order is placed the road is locked ("Locked: the order is placed with this road.").
  - A business is always Regular ("A business buys on the regular road, at its own price.").
  - **Known gap:** the **GL** button at the top does not take a case that has no agency yet: it
    answers "Pick the agency that gives the letter." and the agency list only appears once the
    road is GL. Use Case details for that switch.
- **Screen:** `/leads/133` as `online1` (road box at the top right, locked with a padlock
  because the order is placed). Senior / PWD papers: `/leads/2?step=docs` (a lost case on the
  local copy).

  - A closed (lost) case must be reopened before its details change.

### 8. Say who's buying: a patient or a business

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** A drugstore, hospital, clinic, distributor or government office orders through the
  Online desk. Before the order.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open step 1 **Enquiry & quotation**. At the top is **Who's buying**.
  2. Click **A business (B2B)**.
  3. Pick the kind: **Drugstore / pharmacy**, **Hospital**, **Doctor / clinic**,
     **Distributor**, **Government**.
  4. Type two or more letters in "Find them on file: name, code or number" and click the
     business. If it is not on file, click **New <kind>: add “<case name>” with the order**.
  5. Check the line beside the switch, for example "Drug Central Pharmacy · drugstore price ·
     counts as Online's". **Change** picks another; **A patient** goes back to the patient.
  6. Save the quotation again (procedure 9): the lines are priced from the business's list.
- **What happens next:** The road becomes Regular and any points come off. No prescription is
  asked. When the order is placed, a new business is added as a customer for Management to check.
  The order is the agent's, on the Online channel.
- **Good to know:**
  - Price lists: drugstore, hospital, doctor / clinic and distributor each have their own;
    government buys at the bidding price.
  - A business on file can pay on its own terms ("On their terms (30 days)") besides cash on
    delivery or in advance.
  - Businesses never earn points. Not on PAP cases. Locked once the order is placed.
- **Screen:** `/leads/134?step=quote` as `online1`, scroll to "Who's buying".

### 9. Make and save the quotation

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** As soon as you know the medicine and quantity.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open step 1 **Enquiry & quotation**. "Asked for:" shows the request.
  2. Click **Select medicine** (the list opens) or type a name, generic or strength, and pick it.
  3. On each line set the **quantity** (pieces; − and + move by a pack) and check the **price
     each** (filled with the list price, "Patient price").
  4. If you lower a price, the line says "Below the patient price ₱…" and asks **Why below the
     patient price (Management reads it)**. Type the reason.
  5. Take a line off with the X.
  6. Check the totals: **Medicines**, and on Senior / PWD **VAT removed** and **20% senior
     citizen / PWD discount**; then **To pay** (or **Quotation for the agency** on a GL case).
  7. Click **Save quotation**. It then reads **Saved**.
  8. Click **Next: Rx & documents** (on a GL case **Next: 1.1 Agency application**).
- **What happens next:** Activity says "Quotation saved: … ". The card shows the medicine and
  moves on in the step line. Lines marked **Rx** need a prescription; **Out of stock** warns you.
- **Good to know:**
  - Only medicines in the catalogue, one line per medicine.
  - A price below the list always needs a reason, and Management sees it when it checks the order.
  - A medicine with no price for this buyer: "… has no … price yet. Ask Inventory to set one."
  - On a GL case pick the agency first ("Pick the agency first: the quotation is made for it.").
  - Saving again clears the "sent" mark. Once the order is placed the quotation is locked.
- **Screen:** `/leads/134?step=quote` as `online1` (an ordered case, so the lines are read-only).

  - On a GL case the patient applies to the agency with this quotation.
- **Screen:** `/leads/134?step=quote` as `online1`, scroll to "The quotation for the customer".

### 11. Collect the papers (prescription, IDs, GL papers)

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** After the quotation, as the customer sends their papers (in the chat, by email, or
  in person).
- **Steps:**
  1. Open step 2 **Rx & documents**. Each paper the road needs shows **On file**, **Missing**
     or **Not needed on this road**.
  2. To add a photo or PDF you have: click **Upload** on that paper (**Add another** for more),
     pick one or more files.
  3. Photos that came in the chat wait in **From the chat, not filed yet**. Click the thumbnail
     to read it, then click the paper it is (the missing papers are offered as buttons) or pick
     it in **File as…** (any paper, or Other paper).
  4. Senior / PWD: after the ID is filed, mark which card it is: **Senior citizen** or **PWD**.
  5. For a paper not on the list (PWD purchase booklet, Other paper…), click **File another
     paper**.
  6. A wrong paper: click the bin to take it off.
  7. Click **Next: Order & delivery**.
- **What happens next:** The card's **Papers 1/2** count and the next line update. When the
  required papers are on file the case reaches **Ready** and the next line says "Place the order".
- **Good to know:**
  - Filing a paper means you read and checked it.
  - Photos or PDFs only, up to 10 MB; photos are made smaller by themselves.
  - A paper taken off is kept in the history, marked inactive.
  - The papers travel with the order; Management checks the prescription and the ID.
  - Once the order is placed the papers are locked ("The order is placed with these papers: they
    stay.").
  - The guarantee letter's photo is filed on step 1.2, the pick-up photo on step 4 after
    delivery.
  - A business buying needs no paper ("This road needs no paper before the quotation.").
- **Screen:** `/leads/133?step=docs` as `online1` (Prescription on file, Patient's valid ID
  missing).

### 12. Use the customer's points, and look them up

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** A regular-road patient with points wants them off the order; or someone asks their
  balance.
- **Steps (on the case):**
  1. Open step 3 **Order & delivery** before placing the order. The green box shows
     "Points: N · ₱N to use" and **History**.
  2. Type the points in **Use on this order**, or click **All N**, then **Save**.
  3. To remove them, click **Take off**.
- **Steps (look up):**
  1. Menu **Points**. The top says whether the points program is on and the rate.
  2. Search by name or phone ("Search name or phone…"). Each customer shows the balance,
     earned and used.
  3. Click a customer to see the **History** (earned on which order, used, given back,
     adjusted, by whom).
- **What happens next:** The points show as a line on the order summary and come off the balance
  when the order is placed; the invoice is paid that much "by points" at once.
- **Good to know:**
  - 1 point = ₱1, off the medicines only (never the delivery fee).
  - Regular road only: "Points don't combine with the senior / PWD discount or a guarantee
    letter." The case needs a phone number. Never more than the balance or the medicines' total.
  - A patient earns the program's rate (10% now) of what they actually paid for medicines, once
    the order is delivered and paid; nothing on what they paid with points. Businesses never earn.
    Points never expire. One account per phone number.
  - A cancelled order gives the points back as points, never as money.
  - Only the Admin and the CEO adjust points by hand or switch the program.
  - Points used at a website's checkout come with the website order ("The customer used N points
    at the website's checkout.").
- **Screen:** `/points` as `online1` (on the local copy the program is off and no one has
  points). The case box: `/leads/<id>?step=order` on a regular case with points, before the
  order.

### 13. Place the order

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** The next line says **Place the order** (quotation saved, papers on file; on GL the
  letter verified).
- **Steps:**
  1. Open step 3 **Order & delivery**.
  2. Read **Once placed, it goes**: 1 Management approves → 2 Finance checks (the payment / what
     they owe / the agency's bill) → 3 Dispatch packs and ships. "Management will see: …" lists
     any price below the list.
  3. Check the summary: medicines, senior / PWD relief, a website's delivery fee, points, and
     **To pay** (or **Billed to the agency**).
  4. Under **How they pay** pick **Cash on delivery**, **Paid in advance**, or for a business on
     file **On their terms (…)**. (A GL case has no choice: the agency is billed.)
  5. Click **Place the order**.
- **What happens next:**
  - The patient is found by phone number or added as a new customer (to review); a new business
    is added for Management to check. The case's papers go onto the order.
  - The order (2MG-…) goes to **Management**, then **Finance**, then **Dispatch**. Every desk sees
    it as "Online · Messenger" (or its source).
  - Activity says "Order … placed: …". The customer is emailed "Order confirmed" once Management
    approves.
- **Good to know:**
  - Not ready yet: "Not ready to order: …" says what is missing.
  - A website order arrives with the customer's payment choice already picked and its delivery
    fee.
  - After placing, the road, quotation, buyer, points and papers are locked, and **Close as lost**
    is gone (cancel the order instead).
  - Paid in advance: add the payment slip on the order (procedure 14).
- **Screen:** `/leads/<id>?step=order` on a case at **Ready** (none on the local copy);
  `/leads/133?step=order` shows the same step after placing.

### 14. Paid in advance: put the payment slip on the order

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** The customer paid before delivery and sent the slip (bank or GCash screenshot).
- **Steps:**
  1. On the case, step 3, click **Open the order**.
  2. Under **Where the order is**, click **Finance check**.
  3. Click **+ Proof of payment** and choose the photo.
- **What happens next:** Finance checks the slip against the account, records the money, makes
  the order "paid" and lets it go to Dispatch. The customer is emailed "Payment received".
- **Good to know:**
  - Finance needs the slip or a bank reference to confirm.
  - **Known gap:** while the order waits for Finance, the case says "With Finance: record the
    advance payment received" and may offer **Record payment received**; recording it yourself is
    refused ("… is paid in advance: confirm the money on the order's payment check."). Add the
    slip and let Finance confirm. Record only a balance left after Finance's check.
- **Screen:** `/orders/265?step=finance` as `online1` (the order of case 133).

### 15. Follow the order on the case (and fix it if held or sent back)

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** Every day until the order is delivered and paid. Cases with an order sit in the
  **To deliver** queue.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open the case, step 3. The heading says "Order 2MG-… · ₱… ·" and where it is: Waiting for
     Management, With Finance, With Dispatch, Packed, On the way, Delivered, On hold, Sent back.
  2. Read the trail, row by row: **Management check** (not needed / approved by … / waiting, with
     the reasons / On hold / Sent back), **Finance check** (payment checked or let go by … /
     waiting / On hold by Finance / after Management), **Packed** (who, warehouse, batch),
     **Shipped** (courier and tracking, or rider), **Delivered** / **Delivery · your step**.
     Under it: Receiver, How it pays, Address.
  3. To tell the customer where it is, click **Send to customer** and pick Email, WhatsApp,
     Viber, SMS, Messenger (chat cases) or Copy text.
  4. **On hold** or **Sent back**: the case is in **Waiting on you** with the reason. Click
     **Open the order**:
     - Sent back: **Fix and resubmit** (change the order) or **↩ Resubmit as it is**.
     - On hold: **↩ Re-submit** with your answer, or **Cancel order**.
- **What happens next:** Each step moves the case's next line on. The customer is emailed by
  itself at **Order confirmed**, **On the way**, **Delivered** and **Payment received**.
- **Good to know:**
  - **Orders on 09xx…** under the case header lists every order of this customer.
  - A cancelled order: step 3 says "Order … was cancelled. A new one can be placed."
- **Screen:** `/leads/133?step=order` as `online1` (Management check waiting, with its reasons).

### 16. Hand it over: mark it delivered

- **Who:** Online desk agent (on a case's order). Dispatch can also do it.
- **When:** Dispatch has shipped it and the customer has it, or it was packed for the customer
  to pick up at the warehouse.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open the case, step 3. The **Delivery** row says "your step".
  2. Click **Mark as delivered** (or **Picked up by the customer** when it was packed for
     pick-up).
  3. In **Delivered · 2MG-…** fill **Received by** (the name on the signed receipt) and
     **Delivered on**.
  4. Click **Photo of the signed receipt** and choose the photo.
  5. Optional **Note**. Click **Delivered**.
- **What happens next:** The order is completed. Cash on delivery falls due that day
  ("Cash on delivery: ₱… to collect; the Online desk records it."). The customer is emailed
  "Delivered". The case moves to **Payment** (Delivered, unpaid), or to Done if already paid.
  **Next: Payment** appears.
- **Good to know:**
  - No photo, no delivery: "Add the photo of the signed delivery receipt first: it is the proof
    the customer has it."
  - The day can't be in the future.
  - On a PAP case Dispatch marks it delivered.
- **Screen:** `/leads/<id>?step=order` on a shipped case order (none on the local copy).

### 17. Record the payment received

- **Who:** Online desk agent (on a case's order). On a PAP case, Finance.
- **When:** Cash on delivery after the handover; the agency's money on an Online GL case; any
  balance left on an advance order after Finance's check.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open step 4 **Payment & follow-up** (GL: **Claim & payment**). It shows **Invoice**,
     **Total**, **Received**, **Still owed**, and the payments so far.
  2. Click **Record payment received**.
  3. In **Record payment · INV-…**: **Amount received** (filled with what is owed); **How**
     (Bank transfer, Cash, Check, GCash / Maya, Other); **Paid on**; **Into account** (pick the
     bank, cash or e-wallet account); **Reference (bank or GCash reference)** (for a check:
     **Check number and bank**); **Slip (optional)**: pick one on the order or **Add photo**;
     **Note (optional)**.
  4. Only if the customer withheld tax or deducted something: **Tax withheld, 2307 (optional)**
     with **Form 2307 number**, or **Deducted by them (optional)** with **Why** and **Their debit
     memo #**.
  5. Click **Record payment**.
- **What happens next:** When the invoice is paid in full, the case is **Done** ("Delivered and
  paid; refill when due"), the customer is emailed "Payment received", and their points are
  earned.
- **Good to know:**
  - Never more than is owed. Cash on delivery only after delivery ("… is cash on delivery: record
    the money once the goods are delivered."). Before delivery the step says "Payment comes after
    the delivery." with **Go to delivery**.
  - Advance payments are Finance's (procedure 14).
- **Screen:** `/leads/133?step=pay` as `online1` (before delivery: "Payment comes after the
  delivery." and the refill card).

### 18. Set the next refill, and work Refills due

- **Who:** Online desk agent (the one who took the order).
- **When:** Once the order is placed: ask the patient when their medicines run out.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open step 4 **Payment & follow-up**. The card **Follow-up · next refill** shows the day,
     either "Set by …" or "Suggested: last order … + their usual 28 days".
  2. Click **Set the day** (or **Change**). "When do their medicines run out? Ask them, or count
     the tablets."
  3. Click **15 days**, **30 days**, **60 days** or **90 days**, or pick the **Day**. Add a
     **Note (optional)** such as "30 tablets, 1 a day".
  4. Click **Save**. (**Use the usual gap** removes a set day; **Cancel** leaves it.)
  5. Later, menu **Refills due** (the count beside it): tabs **In 2 days**, **Due now**,
     **Late**, **At risk**, **Lost**. Each patient shows the last order, usual gap, expected day
     and phone. Call them; on Home use **Call** or **New order**.
- **What happens next:** The day counts until the patient's next order and puts them on Refills
  due and Home. The case's Activity notes "Next refill set for …".
- **Good to know:**
  - The day can be from today to one year ahead. It can also be set on the **Customer page**.
  - Refills due lists your own patients: ones you added, ordered for, or whose case is yours.
  - **New order** (Home, Refills due) opens the plain order form. That order has no case, so
    Dispatch marks it delivered and Finance records its money. To keep the whole road on a case,
    start a **New lead** instead.
- **Screen:** `/leads/133?step=pay` as `online1` (refill set, "Runs out tomorrow"); `/to-call` as
  `online1`.

### 20. Close a case as lost, and reopen it

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** The customer will not buy (no answer, price, bought elsewhere…), before an order is
  placed.
- **Steps (close):**
  1. Under the steps, in **Case actions**, click **Close as lost**.
  2. Type **Why it is lost** (required) and click **Close as lost** (or **Keep it open**).
- **Steps (reopen):**
  1. Leads & enquiries → **Lost**, open the case.
  2. Click **Reopen** in Case actions.
- **What happens next:** A lost case leaves Active and shows "Lost: <reason>"; its history stays.
  Reopened, it goes back to its step.
- **Good to know:**
  - With an order placed you can't close the case: "The order is placed. Cancel the order first
    if it isn't going ahead." Management cancels; you can cancel only an order sent back to you or
    held before Finance. A cancelled order lets you close the case or place a new order.
  - A lost Messenger or email case opens again by itself when they write ("They wrote again: the
    case is open again.").
  - A website cancelling its order closes a case not ordered yet.
  - Only the Online desk reopens; Management and the CEO see the button but are refused.
- **Screen:** `/leads/4` as `online1` (a lost case with **Reopen**); `/leads?queue=lost`.

### 21. A guarantee-letter case (apply, letter, verify, claim)

- **Who:** Online desk agent (the PAP officer works the same steps; see procedure 22).
- **When:** The patient pays with a letter from DSWD, PCSO, OVP or OP.
- **Steps:**
  1. Road **Guarantee letter** with the agency (New lead or Case details).
  2. **1 Enquiry & quotation**: quote and **Save quotation** ("Quotation for the agency").
     For **PCSO**: in **PCSO's 3 quotations: ours, and two other vendors'**, type each other
     vendor's name and total from their written quotation and **Save**. The PDF prints all three.
  3. Send the quotation to the patient (procedure 10): they apply to the agency with it.
  4. **1.1 … application** (`?step=apply`): pick **Applied on** → **Record the application** →
     **Next: 1.2 Guarantee letter**.
  5. **1.2 Guarantee letter** (`?step=letter`), when the letter comes: **Upload** the **Photo of
     the letter**; type **Letter number**, **Amount granted (₱)**, **Letter dated**, **Valid
     until**; **Save**. Check it, then **Mark the letter verified** (**Not verified** undoes it).
     → **Next: Rx & documents**.
  6. **2 Rx & documents**: Prescription, Patient's valid ID, Medical certificate or clinical
     abstract.
  7. **3 Order & delivery**: no payment choice ("Billed to the agency"); **Place the order**.
     It goes Management → Finance checks the agency's bill → Dispatch.
  8. Mark it delivered when the patient has it (procedure 16).
  9. **4 Claim & payment**:
     - **4.1 Pick-up photo**: **Upload** "The patient with the medicines".
     - **4.2 Claim sent to …**: **Sent on**, **Claim reference (optional)**, **Claim sent**.
     - **4.3 … pays**: when the agency pays, **Record payment received** (procedure 17).
- **What happens next:** The next line walks you through: "Send the quotation and record the PCSO
  application" → "Waiting for the PCSO letter: follow up" → "Check the guarantee letter and mark
  it verified" → "Place the order" → "Delivered: add the pick-up photo, then send the claim to
  PCSO" → "Claim sent: waiting for PCSO to pay" → Done.
- **Good to know:**
  - Verified needs the letter number, its photo filed, and the amount ("Type the letter's number
    before marking it verified.", "File the photo of the letter before marking it verified.").
  - An expired letter is refused ("That letter has expired: get a new one."). Applied-on and
    letter dates can't be in the future.
  - A letter for less than the quotation holds the order: "The quotation (₱…) is more than the
    letter (₱…): lower the quotation". A letter that expires after verifying: "The letter expired
    on …: get a new letter and record it".
  - The claim needs the pick-up photo first ("Add the pick-up photo before sending the claim."),
    and the photo only after delivery.
  - No points on a GL case. The agency is billed with 60 days.
  - Today a case that turns GL stays with its Online agent; whether it should go to the PAP officer
    is still an open question.
- **Screen:** `/leads/4?step=letter` and `/leads/4?step=quote` as `online1` (a lost PCSO case on
  the local copy, read-only, showing the 3-quotation box).

### 22. The PAP officer's case (PAP desk)

- **Who:** PAP officer (Patient Assistance Program Officer). The PAP head approves the orders;
  Management and the CEO can read the cases.
- **When:** A patient comes for medicines paid by a guarantee letter.
- **Steps:**
  1. Menu **PAP cases**, or **New PAP case** at the top.
  2. In **New PAP case**: who is asking and the patient, phone, email (or **No email**), address,
     city, **Came in through** (Referral by default), what they ask for, and under **Guarantee
     letter from** the agency: DSWD, PCSO, OVP or OP. Click **Open the case**.
  3. Work it like procedure 21: quotation (PCSO's 3 quotations), 1.1 application, 1.2 letter,
     papers on step 2.
  4. **Place the order**. "Once placed, it goes": **The PAP head approves** → Finance checks the
     agency's bill → Dispatch packs and ships.
  5. Dispatch marks it delivered. Then on **4 Claim & payment**: 4.1 pick-up photo, 4.2 **Claim
     sent**. 4.3: Finance records the agency's money on the invoice.
- **What happens next:** The order is the PAP officer's, on the PAP channel. It waits in the PAP
  head's **PAP orders to approve**.
- **Good to know:**
  - The header says **MedRep** (no owner to choose); the road is always the guarantee letter, so
    only the agency can change. There is no Who's buying, no chat and no points.
  - You see only your own PAP cases; the Online desk does not see them.
  - **Known gaps:** **Email the PDF** shows on a PAP case with an email address but is refused
    (only the Online desk may send email): use **Open the PDF** or **Copy its link**, send it
    yourself, then mark **Sent to the customer by**. The case's Activity says "Management checks
    it" after placing, but the PAP head is the one who approves.
- **Screen:** `/pap` as `pap1` (empty on the local copy: "No open PAP case. Add one with New PAP
  case: …"); `/pap?new=1` as `pap1` for the window.

### 23. A website order case

- **Who:** Online desk agent.
- **When:** A customer orders on the 2MG or Caps & Pills website: a **Website order** case
  appears by itself on Leads & enquiries.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open the case. Under the header, click the **Website order …** bar (**Show**) to see what the
     customer saw: items (**Not in the catalogue** marks one the catalogue lacks), Subtotal,
     Discount, Delivery fee, **Total on the website**, how they chose to pay, what they asked for,
     their documents, and "The CRM last told the website: …".
  2. Step 1: the quotation is already made from the website's items at the website's price.
     Check any line not in the catalogue, fix it and **Save quotation**.
  3. Step 2: file the documents they uploaded.
  4. Step 3: **Place the order** (their payment choice is already picked; the delivery fee is on
     the order).
  5. Then follow, deliver and record payment as for any case.
- **What happens next:** The website is told each step by itself: confirmed, shipped (with
  tracking), delivered, cancelled.
- **Good to know:**
  - If the website did not take a status, the bar shows it in red with **Try again**. **Open it in
    the website's admin** opens the order there.
  - A cancel on the website closes a case not ordered yet, or tells you to cancel the order here.
  - Points the customer used at the website's checkout come with the order.
- **Screen:** `/leads?type=website_order` as `online1` (none on the local copy).
