# Management and Finance: how to do each task

Management checks and approves every order. Finance checks the money on every approved order,
collects what comes later, gives money back and pays the suppliers. Both desks work from one
shared screen each, and each person presses Start on it.

Screens below name a page path and the login to take the picture with, on the local copy.
"Management desk screen" and "Finance desk screen" mean the shared screen (the tile, then
Desk screen). "Management own login" means a Management person's own ID.

---

## Part A. Both desks

### Sign in to the shared desk screen
- **Who**: anyone in Management or Finance.
- **When**: at the start of the day, on the desk's computer.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open Bishnoi One. The company portal shows a tile for each workspace.
  2. Press the **Management** tile (or **Finance**).
  3. In the sign-in box, keep the tab on **Desk screen** (the other tab, **My own login**, is for your own things such as payslips and leave).
  4. Type the company's password in **Enter the company's password**. No login ID is needed.
  5. Press **Enter Management** (or **Enter Finance**).
- **What happens next**: the desk's own screen opens. The top bar says **Management desk** (or **Finance desk**) and **Shared desk screen**, not a person's name. The left menu shows **On the desk today** with the desk's people at the top.
- **Good to know**:
  - The password is the company's common password. Ask your lead for it; it is never written down in this book.
  - Signing in does not start anyone. Each person must press **Start** (next task), or what is done is written in the name of the desk's first seat.
  - To leave, press the workspace button at the top right (it signs out and goes back to the tiles), or **Sign out** in the account menu.
- **Screen**: `/login?dept=management`, signed out. Show the box with **Desk screen** picked.

### Start and Stop on the desk
- **Who**: each person on the Management or Finance desk (any of them can press it for a colleague).
- **When**: Start when you sit down to work; Stop when you leave the desk (lunch, a meeting, end of day).
- **Steps**:
  1. Look at **On the desk today · X of Y** at the top of the left menu.
  2. Find your name. If only the people already started are listed, press **+ N not started: Start someone** to see everyone.
  3. Press the green **Start** beside your name.
  4. Your name turns green and shows **since 9:05 AM · 3 open** (the time you started and the orders now yours).
  5. When you leave, press **Stop** beside your name.
- **What happens next**:
  - On **Start**, the waiting orders are shared out again so you get a fair share at once, even of orders already given to others.
  - On **Stop**, every order still open in your name goes back to the desk and is given to the people still started.
- **Good to know**:
  - A Start lasts for today only. Yesterday's Start left open counts as ended; press Start again each morning.
  - Press a name to open that person's page: what they have now and what they did.
  - Only the desk's own people (and the Admin) can press Start or Stop.
- **Screen**: `/` as Management desk screen. The left menu, **On the desk today**.

### How orders are shared out (and taking one by hand)
- **Who**: Management and Finance.
- **When**: all day; it happens by itself.
- **Steps**:
  1. Each new order waiting for your desk is given to one of the people started: whoever has the fewest open, the oldest order first.
  2. You get a message in the bell: **2MG-500058 is yours to check** (Finance: **is yours to check for Finance**).
  3. On the board, each card shows the name of the person who has it.
  4. To work an order nobody has, press **I'll handle it** on its card.
  5. To work an order someone else has (they are busy or away), press **Take over** on its card.
  6. To give one of yours back to the desk, press **Let go** beside your name on the card.
- **What happens next**: the order's timeline says who it was given to, who took it over and who let it go. Whatever is done on the order is written in the name of the person who has it, if that person is started today.
- **Good to know**:
  - A Management person set up in the Admin panel for some Divisions only (Who approves what) is given only those Divisions' orders, and can approve only those.
  - A PAP order is never shared out: the PAP head approves it.
  - An order you took by hand is never moved to someone else to even the load.
- **Screen**: `/` as Management desk screen, the queue under **Waiting for approval**: a card's top right corner with a name and **Take over**.

### Team figures
- **Who**: Management and Finance (the CEO and the Admin also read them).
- **When**: at the end of the day, the week or the month, to see how the desk did.
- **Steps**:
  1. In the menu, press **Team figures**.
  2. Pick **Today**, **This week** or **This month** at the top right.
  3. Read each person's row: **Hours started**, **Given**, **Took over**, **Approved** (Finance: **Let go to Dispatch**), **Sent back** (Management only), **Held**, **Rejected**, **Time per order**, **Late**, **Open now**.
  4. Press a person's name to see every order they had and what they did with each (**What**, **When**, **Took**).
- **What happens next**: nothing changes; it is a report.
- **Good to know**:
  - Time is working time, counted from when the order became that person's.
  - **Late** means over 4 working hours for Management, over a working day for Finance.
  - See "For the team" at the end: the **Rejected** column today counts cancelled orders, and rejected ones are counted under **Held**.
- **Screen**: `/desk/team?range=week` as Management desk screen (and `/desk/team` as Finance desk screen).

## Part B. Management

### Read the board: orders to approve
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: all day. It is Management's Home.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Orders to approve** in the menu (or the Bishnoi One name at the top).
  2. Read the four figures: **Waiting for approval** (with the oldest wait), **On hold**, **Customers to check**, **Orders today**.
  3. Under them, the queue **Waiting for approval** opens on the tab **Needs you**. Other tabs: **Sent back**, **With Finance**, **With Dispatch**, **On hold**, **Cancelled**, **Delivered today**.
  4. Narrow it with the search box (**Search customer, medicine, MedRep or order…**), **All channels** and **Every MedRep**.
  5. On each card, read **Next action**: either **Look at: …** (what needs a look) or **Everything checks out: approve it**.
  6. Read the strip **Management check · N of 4 fine**: four bars, **Price**, **Stock**, **Customer**, **Papers**. Green is fine, amber needs a look. Press a bar to open that check on the order.
  7. Press the card anywhere else to open the order.
- **What happens next**: the order opens on its first check that needs a look.
- **Good to know**:
  - Cards are grouped by the day they were placed. **New** means it came less than an hour ago; **Late** means it waited over 4 working hours; **Changed by Mgmt** means Management changed its medicines or prices.
  - **Medicine check** (top right) shows a medicine's stock in each warehouse, the next batch to go out and what is coming.
  - **Cards**, **Table** and **Board** change the look; your pick is remembered on this computer.
- **Screen**: `/` as Management desk screen, scroll to **Waiting for approval**.

### Check an order: the four checks, then Approve
- **Who**: Management (or a PAP order's head).
- **When**: for every order in **Needs you**.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the order from its card.
  2. On the left, under **MANAGEMENT CHECKS**, see the five steps: **Documents** (for a patient: **RX & documents**), **Medicines & price**, **Stock & warehouse**, **Customer & licence**, **Approve**. A green tick is fine; an amber **!** needs a look; the line under each says why.
  3. The page opens on the first check that needs a look. Read it on the right.
  4. Press **Next: …** at the foot of the panel to go to the next check, or press any check on the left.
  5. On **Approve**, read the four lines again, then press **Approve**, **Send back** or **Reject**.
- **What happens next**: see the tasks below for each check and each decision.
- **Good to know**:
  - Under the checks on the left are the rarer buttons: **Put on hold**, **Mark exception**, **Cancel order**, and **Wait for stock** when a medicine is short.
  - Below them, **Timeline** shows everything that happened, and **Money** shows each medicine, the total and the invoice.
  - The system never stops you approving, except a patient's order with no prescription. Everything else is your call.
- **Screen**: `/orders/232?step=management` as Management desk screen.

### Check 1: Documents (read the papers by hand)
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: on every order that has papers on file.
- **Steps**:
  1. On the order, press **Documents** (for a patient: **RX & documents**) on the left.
  2. Read the grey hint if there is one, for example **Next: add Prescription.**
  3. Under **Papers**, open each paper on file (press its name or picture). For a prescription, check the right patient, the doctor, the medicine and the date.
  4. If a paper is missing and you have it, press **Add photo** on its row (or **Add another**).
  5. When every paper is right, press **Papers are right** in the amber band.
- **What happens next**: the band turns green: **Papers checked by hand by (name), (date).** The **Papers** bar on the card turns green. The timeline says so.
- **Good to know**:
  - The system never ticks papers by itself; a person reads them.
  - Pressed by mistake? Press **Take back**.
  - If anyone adds a new paper later (not a payment slip or a delivery receipt), the check is cleared and must be done again.
  - A hospital's own purchase order (**No PO from them**) is for information only, never a stop.
  - A patient's order cannot be approved without the prescription: attach it, or send the order back for it.
  - The proof of payment is Finance's to check, not Management's.
- **Screen**: `/orders/265?step=management&check=papers` as Management desk screen. Scroll to **Papers are right**.

### Check 2: Medicines and price (the last price given, a price below list)
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: on every order; it needs a look when a price differs from the list or something is free.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Medicines & price** on the left.
  2. Read the amber lines at the top. A price below list reads like: **AlisLergy 10 mg: given at ₱10.80, Patient price is ₱12.00 (₱120.00 less in all). Reason: Matched the pharmacy's price.** The reason is the MedRep's.
  3. In the table, compare **List price** with **Given**. A lower price is amber with **−10% vs list** under it.
  4. Under each medicine, read **Last time**: the price this customer got last, the pieces, the day and the order. A lower price than last time is amber.
  5. Press the **N orders** chip to see every earlier price, with **Lowest**, **Highest** and **Average**.
  6. Use **What they paid before** (**Strip**, **Steps**, **Open**) to change how the earlier prices show.
  7. Decide: the price is fine (go on), you change it (see "Change the order's medicines"), you need a yes from above (see "Hold with a question"), or you send it back.
- **What happens next**: nothing changes until you decide.
- **Good to know**:
  - Free goods typed by the MedRep (not an offer's) show as **Free goods** for you to agree to.
  - A medicine priced as another kind of buyer (a distributor's price given to a hospital) is flagged too.
  - A senior citizen's or PWD's 20% off is below the list by law and is not flagged.
  - An Online order is priced as quoted on its case: **Open the quotation** shows it.
  - Press a medicine's name to see its prices, stock in each warehouse and batches.
- **Screen**: `/orders/265?step=management&check=price` as Management desk screen. Scroll to **Last time**.

### Check 3: Stock and warehouse (stock short)
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: on every order; it needs a look when a medicine is short or a cold medicine has no cold box.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Stock & warehouse** on the left.
  2. In the table, read **Free stock**: what is on the shelf less what earlier orders hold. Red means short.
  3. Under **Ships from**, read each warehouse: **Can fill every line**, or what it is short of (**needs 50, has 20**). Pick the warehouse it ships from.
  4. If it is short everywhere, choose one:
     - press **Wait for stock** (left, under the checks) to hold it until the stock comes in; or
     - approve it anyway from a warehouse that is short (Dispatch will see it); or
     - send it back so the MedRep changes the quantity.
  5. For a cold medicine with **No cold box**, add the styro box and its price (see "Change the order's medicines").
- **What happens next**: with **Wait for stock**, the order goes **On Hold** with the short lines written in; you and the MedRep are told when the stock comes in, and the order shows **Stock is back… Take it off hold.**
- **Good to know**: **Medicine check** on the board shows every warehouse's stock and what is coming, without opening an order.
- **Screen**: `/orders/232?step=management&check=stock` as Management desk screen. Scroll to **Ships from**. (The local copy has no order short of stock today.)

### Check 4: Customer and licence (new, licence, overdue, credit)
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: on every order.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Customer & licence** on the left.
  2. Read the amber lines: **New customer**, **Licence expired**, **Overdue N days** (with what they owe), **Over credit limit**.
  3. Read the customer box: **Orders**, **Bought**, **Owes now**, **Last order**, **Buying since**, and what they usually take with the last price. A first order says **First order.**
  4. Read the licence line: **LTO number** (a doctor: **PRC licence**), **Expires**, **TIN**. If something is missing, press **Missing …: fill it on the customer** to fix it on the customer's page.
  5. Read **Details** (Division, payment terms, address, day wanted). To correct them, press **Edit details**, fix **Complete the order** and press its save button.
  6. Decide:
     - new customer: check their papers first (see "Customers to check"), then approve;
     - licence expired: hold or send back until a new one is on file;
     - overdue or over the credit limit: approve if you agree, hold with a question, or send back.
- **What happens next**: nothing changes until you decide. Finance sees the same overdue and credit facts again at its own check.
- **Good to know**: the payment terms on the order can be changed here with **Edit details**; the customer's own terms are changed on the customer's page (Finance's task "Set a customer's terms and credit limit").
- **Screen**: `/orders/217?step=management&check=customer` as Management desk screen (licence expired, overdue 66 days).

### Approve and pick the warehouse
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: after the four checks.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Approve** (on the order's **Approve** step, or straight on the card).
  2. The window **Approve and check stock** opens.
  3. Under **Send from warehouse**, pick the warehouse. Each says **Can fill every line** or **Short on N lines**. Others short on a line hide behind **N more, short on a line**.
  4. Read the table for the warehouse picked: **Ordered**, **Free here**, **Enough** or **Short N**.
  5. Leave **Finance checks it too** ticked (untick only when Finance does not need to see it).
  6. Press **Approve**.
- **What happens next**:
  - The invoice is made at once.
  - Paid in advance: the order goes **With Finance** to check the payment.
  - Cash on delivery or credit: it goes to Finance's check (**Finance Check**) to look at what the customer owes.
  - **Finance checks it too** unticked: it goes straight to Dispatch with its invoice.
  - The timeline says **Approved. Ships from (warehouse).**
- **Good to know**:
  - You can approve only orders of the Divisions you approve.
  - Approved by mistake? See the next task.
- **Screen**: `/orders/232?step=management&check=approve&approve=1` as Management desk screen (the window open).

### Take back an approval
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: within 30 minutes of approving, before Finance or Dispatch has done anything with it.
- **Steps**:
  1. On the board, the card shows **Take back approval · N min left**; or on the order, press **Take back approval (until 3:40 PM)** on the left.
  2. Type the reason and press **Take back approval**.
- **What happens next**: the order comes back to **Needs you**. Its invoice is cancelled and made again when it is approved. Finance and Dispatch stop seeing it.
- **Good to know**: after 30 minutes, or once Finance or Dispatch has acted, it cannot be taken back: put it on hold instead.
- **Screen**: `/` as Management desk screen, tab **With Finance**, a card just approved.

### Send back with a reason
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: the MedRep must fix something (a price, a quantity, a paper, the address).
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Send back** (on the card, or on the order's **Approve** step).
  2. In **Send back to the MedRep**, type what to fix under **Reason**.
  3. Press **Send back**.
- **What happens next**: the order goes back to the MedRep as a **Draft** with your reason in a yellow band (**Sent back … What to fix:**). The MedRep presses **Fix and resubmit** or **↩ Resubmit as it is** with an answer. It then comes back to **Needs you** with their answer under it.
- **Good to know**: the orders waiting for a MedRep's fix are under **Stopped orders → Sent back**. The number of times it was sent back shows on the order.
- **Screen**: `/orders/265?step=management&check=approve` as Management desk screen, after pressing **Send back**.

### Hold with a question (ask the Team Lead or Business Mentor)
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: you need a yes from above before deciding (a price far below list, a customer far overdue), or a fact you don't have.
- **Steps**:
  1. On the order, press **Put on hold** on the left.
  2. Under **Reason**, write the question plainly, for example: "12% below the list for this hospital: yes from the Team Lead?"
  3. Press **Put on hold**.
  4. Ask the MedRep's Team Lead or the Business Mentor through **Messages**, naming the order.
  5. When the answer comes, open the order and take it off hold (next task), then approve, send back or reject.
- **What happens next**: the order goes **On Hold**, listed under **Stopped orders → On hold**. The MedRep sees the reason and can answer with **↩ Re-submit**; the answer goes on the timeline and the hold stays until Management takes it off.
- **Good to know**:
  - Bishnoi One has a box **Ask up the line** (**Ask (name) (Team Lead)**, **Ask (name) (Business Mentor)**) where the person asked answers **Yes** or **No** on the order. Today it does not show on Management's order page (see "For the team" at the end), so use the hold and Messages for now.
  - For something Management can't settle alone (a contract, a dispute, a special price for the CEO), use **Mark exception** instead.
- **Screen**: `/orders/206?step=management` as Management desk screen (an order on hold; the red band with the reason).

### Take an order off hold
- **Who**: Management (any hold); Finance takes off only its own holds.
- **When**: the question is answered or the reason is gone.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open **Stopped orders → On hold**, then the order.
  2. Press **Take off hold** on the left of the order (the card's **Take off hold** does the same, without the choice below).
  3. When **Continue from** shows, pick **Where it stopped — …**, or **Back to …** an earlier desk to look at it again.
  4. Under **Why can it go on?**, type why.
  5. Press **Take off hold**.
- **What happens next**: it goes on from the step picked; the MedRep and that desk are told. Sent back to Management's approval, its invoice is cancelled and made again when approved.
- **Good to know**: once money is on its invoice, or it was packed or sent out, it can only go on from where it stopped.
- **Screen**: `/on-hold` as Management desk screen; then `/orders/206`.

### Reject an order
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: the order should not go on as it is, and you want it stopped (not just fixed).
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Reject** (on the card, or on the order's **Approve** step).
  2. In **Reject this order**, type the **Reason**.
  3. Press **Reject**.
- **What happens next**: the order stops **On Hold** with your reason. It is not deleted. Nothing goes further until it is taken off hold. The MedRep can answer, or cancel it.
- **Good to know**: to end an order for good, use **Cancel order** instead.
- **Screen**: `/orders/217?step=management&check=approve` as Management desk screen, the **Reject** window.

### Mark an exception
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: a problem Management can't settle alone: a contract, a dispute, a special price.
- **Steps**:
  1. On the order, press **Mark exception** on the left.
  2. Type the **Reason** and press **Mark exception**.
- **What happens next**: it waits, marked **Exception**, with the held orders, until the CEO or a supervisor decides and Management takes it off hold.
- **Screen**: `/orders/217?step=management` as Management desk screen, the left column.

### Cancel an order
- **Who**: Management (a MedRep may cancel their own draft, or their order held before Finance).
- **When**: the order must end for good: the customer no longer wants it, a duplicate.
- **Steps**:
  1. On the order, press **Cancel order** on the left.
  2. In **Cancel this order**, type the **Reason**.
  3. Press **Cancel order**.
- **What happens next**:
  - The order is **Cancelled** and kept, with its reason.
  - Its invoice is cancelled. Money already received appears in Finance's **Refunds owed**.
  - Packed pieces go back on the shelf; points the customer used go back to them.
- **Good to know**: a parcel **In Transit** can't be cancelled; it must come back first.
- **Screen**: `/orders/217?step=management` as Management desk screen, the **Cancel this order** window.

### Bring back a cancelled order (Restore)
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: an order was cancelled by mistake, or the customer wants it after all.
- **Steps**:
  1. On the board, press the tab **Cancelled** and open the order.
  2. Press **Restore** on the left.
  3. Under **Why can it go on?**, type why, and press **Restore**.
- **What happens next**: the order comes back to **Needs you** as it was. The cancel stays on its timeline. Its invoice is made again when it is approved.
- **Good to know**: **Restore** shows only while no money was given back on it, no points were used, there is no return on it, and it is not an Online case's or a PAP order.
- **Screen**: `/orders/298?step=management` as Management desk screen.

### Change the order's medicines
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: before approving: add a medicine, give some free, change a price or a quantity, take a line off, add a cold box.
- **Steps**:
  1. On the order, press **Medicines & price**, then **Edit medicines · add, free, price**.
  2. To add a medicine, type in **Type a medicine name or code…** and pick it.
  3. Change **Qty** or **Price** in place. When a price is not the list's, type why in the small box (**Why below the list price** or **Why above the list price**).
  4. To give some free, press the gift button (**Give some free (₱0)**) on the line, and type the remark.
  5. To take a line off, press the bin button.
  6. Under **Why you changed it (the MedRep reads it)**, type why.
  7. Press **Save changes**. Read **Confirm changes** (each change, and **Total ₱X → ₱Y**).
  8. Press **Confirm changes** (or **Back to editing**).
- **What happens next**: the order stays with you to approve. The MedRep is told what changed and why. Every version stays on the order, and the card shows **Changed by Mgmt**.
- **Screen**: `/orders/265?step=management&check=price` as Management desk screen, after pressing **Edit medicines · add, free, price**.

### Customers to check (a new customer's papers)
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: a MedRep added a customer from the field. Their orders go through, but Management checks the customer once.
- **Steps**:
  1. In the menu, press **Customers to check** (or the figure **Customers to check** on Home).
  2. The page **New customers to check** lists each one with **New: Management checks it**.
  3. Open a customer.
  4. On the right, under **Licence and papers**, read **Missing: …**. Open each paper on file (**BIR 2303**, **LTO permit**, **Business permit**); attach one you have with **Attach** (or **Add newer**).
  5. Check the name, address, licence number and expiry, and TIN. Fix anything wrong with **Edit details**.
  6. Press **Mark checked** at the top right.
- **What happens next**: the customer is no longer new; their page shows **Checked by (name)**. The **New customer** line disappears from their orders.
- **Good to know**: a patient has no licence papers; check their details and press **Mark checked**.
- **Screen**: `/customers?review=1` as Management desk screen; then `/customers/91939`.

### Returns to approve
- **Who**: Management (Dispatch checks the goods that come back).
- **When**: a MedRep (or Management) asked for a return on a delivered order.
- **Steps**:
  1. In the menu, press **Stopped orders → Returns to approve**. The page **Returns & refunds** opens on **Waiting for Management**.
  2. Read the card: the return's number, the order, the customer, what comes back and how many, the reason, and where the goods are.
  3. Press **Approve** (type **A word (optional)**) or **Don't approve** (type **Why**), then press the button in the window.
  4. When the goods came back and Dispatch checked them, the return moves to **Checked: to credit**. Open that tab and press **Credit ₱X**, or **Don't credit** with why.
- **What happens next**:
  - Goods coming back: Dispatch checks them (good back on the shelf, damaged written off, missing not credited).
  - The customer keeps the goods: the bill is credited when you approve.
  - Not shipped yet: approving cancels the order; money paid goes to **Refunds owed**.
  - A credit lowers the invoice; money paid beyond the new bill shows in Finance's **Refunds owed**.
- **Screen**: `/returns?status=requested` as Management desk screen (empty on the local copy today); `/returns` shows a credited one.

### Stopped orders: sent back, on hold, stuck
- **Who**: Management.
- **When**: once or twice a day, to chase what is not moving.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the menu folder **Stopped orders**.
  2. **Sent back**: orders waiting for a MedRep's fix (page **Sent back to MedReps**).
  3. **On hold**: every order held, by any desk, with its reason.
  4. **Stuck over 2 days**: orders that have not moved for two days at Finance or Dispatch.
  5. **Returns to approve**: see the task above.
  6. Open any order to act on it, or message the person who has it.
- **Screen**: `/orders?stuck=1` as Management desk screen; also `/on-hold` and `/orders/sent-back`.

## Part C. Finance

### Read the Finance check strip on a card
- **Who**: Finance.
- **When**: all day. **Orders to check** is Finance's Home.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Orders to check** in the menu. The tab **Needs you** holds every order waiting for Finance.
  2. On each card, read **Next action**: **Look at: …**, or **Everything checks out: check it and send it on** (prepaid), or **Everything checks out: make the invoice** (credit and cash on delivery).
  3. Read the strip **Finance check · N of 4 fine**:
     - **Payment**: how it pays (**Proof attached: match it with the bank**, **Cash on delivery: ₱X collected at the door**, **On credit: 30 days from delivery**, **Pays by (date)**).
     - **Papers**: the papers on file.
     - **Dues**: **Nothing overdue**, or how long the customer is overdue.
     - **Credit**: **Within the ₱X limit**, **Over credit limit**, **Not on credit**, or grey **On credit, no limit set** (set one; it never stops the order).
  4. Press **Confirm payment** (prepaid) or **Check & send to Dispatch** (credit, cash on delivery) on the card, or open the order first.
- **Good to know**:
  - Other tabs: **Not yet with Finance** (still with Management), **With Dispatch**, **On hold**, **Delivered today**.
  - **Board** shows three columns: **Check payment**, **Check dues**, **Collect**.
  - Use **I'll handle it**, **Take over** and **Let go** as Management does.
- **Screen**: `/` as Finance desk screen.

### Confirm a prepaid order's payment (match the slip with the bank)
- **Who**: Finance.
- **When**: an order paid in advance is approved. It waits in **Prepaid orders** and in **Orders to check**.
- **Steps**:
  1. In the menu, press **Prepaid orders**, then **Confirm payment** on the card (or open the order: **Payment proof**, then **Finance check**, then **Confirm payment**).
  2. In **Confirm payment · (order)**, open each slip under **Proof of payment** (**Added by (name) · open it**).
  3. Look in the bank (or GCash) account that the money arrived: the same amount, day and reference. In Books you can open **Books → Banking**, the account, then **Statement**.
  4. Type **Amount received** (it starts at what is still due).
  5. Pick **How** (**Bank transfer**, **Cash**, **Check**, **GCash / Maya**, **Other**), **Paid on**, and **Into account** (the bank, cash or e-wallet account).
  6. Type **Reference (bank or GCash reference)** (for a check: **Check number and bank**).
  7. Tick **I have checked the proof, and this money is in our account.**
  8. Press **Confirm payment**.
- **What happens next**: the money goes on the order's invoice and the order goes to Dispatch. The timeline says **Payment checked: …**. Paid in full, it says **Paid in full**. A bar at the foot offers **Undo (90s)**.
- **Good to know**:
  - No slip on the order: the window says **No slip on the order** (or the MedRep's reason). You must type the bank reference you checked, or hold it until the slip comes.
  - Less than the total is allowed: the rest stays owed on the invoice. More than due is refused.
  - Already paid (an order brought back after a cancel): confirm with ₱0.00.
  - **Confirmations by this login** (menu, Look up) lists what this screen confirmed.
- **Screen**: `/finance/prepaid` as Finance desk screen (empty on the local copy today); the window opens from a card's **Confirm payment**.

### Check a credit or cash-on-delivery order and send it to Dispatch (the invoice)
- **Who**: Finance.
- **When**: an approved order that pays later (cash on delivery, 30, 45 or more days, or by a date).
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the order from **Orders to check** (status **Finance Check**). It opens on **Finance check**.
  2. Read the box on what the customer owes: **Nothing owed from before: clear to send on**, **Owes ₱X from before, nothing late**, or **₱X overdue, the oldest N days late** (with **Collect it first, or ask Management.**), and whether this order puts them over their credit limit.
  3. Press **Their whole history** if you need the customer's page.
  4. Read the invoice line: **Invoice**, **Made**, **Due**.
  5. Press **Check & send to Dispatch** (also on the card). If it should not go yet, press **Put on hold** on the left instead.
- **What happens next**: the order goes to Dispatch (**Ready For Dispatch**). The money comes later and shows in **Due payments**.
- **Good to know**:
  - The invoice is made when Management approves; Finance's press is the check that lets it go. Its number is in the company's own series (2MG Inc. or GetMeds Philippines Inc.).
  - Several orders with nothing found: press **Invoice the N with nothing found** at the top of **Orders to check** to send them all.
  - Pressed by mistake: **Undo** shows beside **Checked by (name)** for 90 seconds.
- **Screen**: `/orders/214?step=finance` as Finance desk screen (overdue 4 days).

### Hold an order at Finance, and take it off
- **Who**: Finance.
- **When**: no slip, the money is not in the bank, the customer must pay old dues first.
- **Steps**:
  1. Press **Hold** on the card (or **Put on hold** on the order's left).
  2. Type the **Reason**.
  3. Under **Screenshots for the MedRep (up to 5)**, press **Add screenshot** to add what you saw (the bank page, the slip).
  4. Press **Put on hold**.
  5. To let it go on, press **Take off hold** on the card or the order. No reason is asked.
- **What happens next**: the order waits **On Hold** with the MedRep told why, with your screenshots. If you held it for want of the slip, adding the proof of payment sends it back to Finance by itself.
- **Good to know**: Finance takes off only its own holds; Management can take off any.
- **Screen**: `/` as Finance desk screen, the **Put on hold** window from a card.

### Due payments by terms, and Mark paid
- **Who**: Finance.
- **When**: every day, when money comes in for an order paid later.
- **Steps**:
  1. In the menu, press **Due payments**.
  2. Press a box at the top for the terms: **Cash on delivery**, **30 days**, **45 days**, **By a date**, … and **From before** (old invoices) and **Collected**. Each shows the amount, the orders and how many are late.
  3. Find the order (search **Search customer, order, invoice or seller**). Late ones are first, then **Due**, then **Not delivered yet**.
  4. Read **Delivered** and **To pay by**. A credit order not yet delivered shows **After delivery**.
  5. Press **Mark paid** on the row.
  6. Fill **Record payment** (see the next task) and press **Record payment**.
- **What happens next**: paid in full, the order is done (delivered and paid) and moves to **Collected** (the last 30 days, with **Paid on** and **Marked by**).
- **Good to know**:
  - Credit days count from delivery, not from the invoice: a 30-day order delivered on 19 November is due on 19 December.
  - Cash on delivery is due on the day it is delivered and never late before.
  - **From before** rows are old Zoho invoices: their money is recorded in Books (**In Books**).
  - A cash-on-delivery row **Not delivered yet** also shows **Mark paid**, but it is refused until the delivery is marked (see "For the team").
  - **Money → Customers owe** shows each customer's dues by how late.
- **Screen**: `/collect` as Finance desk screen (tab **30 days**).

### Record a payment on an invoice (tax withheld 2307, deductions)
- **Who**: Finance (the Online desk for its own cases' orders).
- **When**: money came in for a credit or delivered cash-on-delivery invoice, or the rest of an advance.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the invoice (**Money → Invoices**, or the order's **Paid** step, or **Mark paid** in **Due payments**). Press **Record payment** (on the order: **Record payment received**).
  2. Read **Still owed**.
  3. Type **Amount received**: the money that actually came in.
  4. If the customer withheld tax, type it in **Tax withheld, 2307 (optional)** and the **Form 2307 number**.
  5. If the customer deducted something, type it in **Deducted by them (optional)**, pick **Why** (**Near-expiry goods kept**, **Listing fee**, **Promo / rebate**, **Penalty**, **Other**) and type **Their debit memo #**.
  6. Check the line **Settles ₱X of the ₱Y owed**.
  7. Pick **How**, **Paid on**, **Into account**, and type the **Reference**.
  8. Under **Slip (optional)**, pick the slip on the order, or press **Add photo**.
  9. Press **Record payment**.
- **What happens next**: the money, the tax withheld and the deduction each go on the invoice and settle it. The timeline and **Payments received** show it. Paid in full and delivered, the order is **Completed: delivered and paid.**
- **Good to know**:
  - The Form 2307 number is required when tax is withheld; **Why** is required when something is deducted.
  - Money, tax and deduction together can't be more than what is owed.
  - Cash on delivery: record it only once delivered. Advance: confirm it on the order's payment check instead.
  - The tax withheld and the deduction are never given back as money.
- **Screen**: `/invoices/171` as Finance desk screen; the **Record payment** window.

### Use the customer's credit on an invoice
- **Who**: Finance.
- **When**: the customer has credit (an old credit note, or money kept from a bill) and an open invoice.
- **Steps**:
  1. On the order's **Paid** step, press **Use their credit (₱X)**.
  2. Under **From**, pick the credit (**Credit note …** or **Kept from …**).
  3. Type **Amount to use** and press **Use the credit**.
- **What happens next**: the invoice owes that much less; the credit left goes down.
- **Good to know**: the customer's page shows **Credit · ₱X** with each source.
- **Screen**: `/orders/214?step=paid` as Finance desk screen (shows only when the customer has credit).

### Take off a payment recorded by mistake
- **Who**: Finance.
- **When**: a payment was typed on the wrong invoice or with the wrong amount.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the invoice. Under **Payments received**, press **Take off** on the wrong line.
  2. In **Take off this payment**, type **Why** and press **Take it off**.
- **What happens next**: the line stays in the history, struck through and marked **Taken off**; the invoice is owed again. Record the right payment after.
- **Good to know**: on a cancelled invoice, give the money back from **Refunds owed** instead.
- **Screen**: `/invoices/171` as Finance desk screen, **Payments received**.

### Refunds owed: give money back
- **Who**: Finance.
- **When**: an order was cancelled after the customer paid, or a return or change left the customer paid beyond the bill.
- **Steps**:
  1. In the menu, press **Refunds owed**.
  2. Read each row: **Cancelled** (with **Waiting N days**), **Order**, **Customer**, **Why**, **Received**, **Given back**, **To give back**.
  3. Press **Give back**.
  4. In **Money owed back · (order)**, choose **Give the money back** or **Keep as their credit**.
  5. Type the amount (**Amount given back** or **Amount kept as credit**).
  6. Giving money back: pick **How**, **Given back on**, **Out of account**, and type the **Reference**.
  7. Press **Record refund** (or **Keep as credit**).
- **What happens next**: the row leaves the list when all is settled and shows under **Given back**. Kept as credit, no money goes out; it is used on the customer's next invoice.
- **Good to know**: a cancelled invoice's page also has **Give money back**. Points and tax withheld are never given back as money.
- **Screen**: `/refunds` as Finance desk screen.

### Set a customer's terms and credit limit
- **Who**: Finance or Management (and the Admin).
- **When**: a customer moves to credit, gets more days, or needs a limit.
- **Steps**:
  1. Open the customer (**Customers** in the menu, or the customer's name on an order).
  2. In the **Money** box on the right, press **Change terms or credit limit**.
  3. In **How (customer) pays**, pick **Payment terms**: **Pays in advance**, **Cash on delivery** or **On credit**.
  4. On credit, type **Days to pay, from delivery** (for example 30 or 45).
  5. Type **Credit limit (₱, empty for none)**, or leave it empty for no limit.
  6. Press **Save**.
- **What happens next**: new orders take these terms. Orders already placed keep their own. The top of the page shows **Pays** and **Credit limit**, and **Credit left**.
- **Good to know**: Finance's strip shows **On credit, no limit set** in grey until a limit is set.
- **Screen**: `/customers/61614` as Finance desk screen, the **Change terms or credit limit** window.

### Supplier bills to pay
- **Who**: Finance (Inventory and Finance can enter a bill; only Finance pays).
- **When**: a supplier's bill falls due.
- **Steps**:
  1. In the menu, press **Supplier bills to pay**. The page **Bills** opens.
  2. Press the tab **To pay** or **Overdue**.
  3. Open the bill. Check the vendor, the lines and **Still to pay**.
  4. Press **Record payment**.
  5. In **Record a payment to a vendor**, type **Amount paid (₱)**, **Paid on**, **How**, **Paid from (account)** and **Reference**.
  6. Under **Their unpaid bills**, type in **Put on it** how much goes on each bill (the bill you came from is filled in).
  7. Check **Paid**, **On bills** and **Left as an advance**. Add **Notes (for us only)** if needed.
  8. Press **Save the payment**.
- **What happens next**: the bills paid show **Paid** (or **Part paid**). Money paid beyond the bills stays as an advance with the vendor, to use later with **Use on bills**.
- **Good to know**:
  - A new bill (rent, a utility, freight): **New bill** on the **Bills** page.
  - A bill with nothing paid on it can be voided with **Void**.
  - **Purchases → Payments made** lists every payment to vendors.
- **Screen**: `/purchases/bills?view=open` as Finance desk screen.
