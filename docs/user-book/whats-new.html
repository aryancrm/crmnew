What's better, and what now runs by itself

One system for 2MG and GetMeds: selling, orders, money, stock and people. Every seat has one login and sees only its own work.

Better than before

  • One login per seat. Each menu is built around that person's work: their own work on top, look-ups below. Someone who moves seat gets the new seat's screens and data.
  • Create New Order for every seller, in five parts. The Division comes from the seat, so an order can't go under the wrong Division or the wrong team lead.
  • Management checks every order by hand, with four checks shown on the order: papers, price (against the list and the last price given), stock and warehouse, and the customer (licence, overdue, credit limit).
  • "Where the order is": the seller sees every step live (Management, Finance, packing, on the road, delivered, paid).
  • Finance by terms: cash on delivery, 30, 45 or any number of days; due payments by terms, statement of account, refunds owed, tax withheld (2307).
  • Dispatch: packs from the warehouse by batch, nearest expiry first. Ships by courier or our own rider, with tracking, and closes the order with the signed receipt.
  • Stock and buying inside, not in Zoho: medicines, prices for each kind of buyer, stock by warehouse and batch, transfers and counts. Buying runs purchase order → receive → vendor's bill → pay.
  • Online desk: every Messenger, website and email enquiry is a case. It goes from quotation to papers, order, payment and refill on one page, with the chat beside it.
  • Field work: visit plan, check-in and call report, expenses with receipts, samples, the evening huddle, scorecards and where the team is.
  • HR: Start my day, who's working, leave and forms, payroll and payslips.
  • CEO Home: the business first (sales, collected, owed, orders, gross profit, medicines, customers), then the people (who's in, late, on leave).
  • Messages inside the system replace emails between departments; announcements show who has read them.

Now automatic: nobody types it

  • The target sheet builds itself from the invoices (VAT out), to any day, in the same layout as the Excel, with totals and colours. The Excel tracker is no longer needed.
  • Refills and reorders: the system learns how often each customer orders. It lists who is due (Go here first, Hospitals to call, Refills due), with a badge on the menu.
  • Orders go to the right people: the seller's team lead and head see them by the company tree. Desk work is shared out to whoever pressed Start.
  • Warnings before approval: a price below the list, a price that changed from last time, low stock, an expired licence, money overdue, or a customer over their credit limit.
  • The invoice is made when Management approves. The customer gets an email at each step when their email is on file.
  • Stock is held for the order when it is placed. A cancel frees it for the next order waiting.
  • Late payments are counted by terms and shown to Finance with a badge; customers who owe show to their seller.
  • Every seller's numbers: sales against target, days to collect, customers not reordered, and the invoice on the last working day.
  • Attendance from Start my day: on time, late or not in, for HR, the team lead and the CEO.
  • Buy again: medicines about to run out are listed for buying, with each vendor's last price.
  • Batch trace: for a complaint or recall, the customers a batch reached, in one search.
  • Data checks: the system checks its own books (bills, payments, stock) against itself.
Rules the system keeps: Management approves every order · Rx for every patient purchase · one seat, one Division · a price below the list needs a reason · nothing is deleted (cancel, return, credit: the history stays) · the role is checked on every action, not just the button.
