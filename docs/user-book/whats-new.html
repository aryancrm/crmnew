Bishnoi One: what changed

One system instead of the hub, Zoho, the old CRM, the Excel target tracker and SEFMED.

1. What happens by itself now

  • The target sheet is made from the invoices every day. Nobody fills the Excel.
  • The invoice is made when Management approves the order.
  • The order goes to the seller's own team lead and head by itself.
  • Stock is held for the order when it is placed. If the order is cancelled, the stock is freed.
  • The system knows how often each customer orders and lists who is due to reorder.
  • Late payments are counted by each customer's terms (cash on delivery, 30, 45 or any number of days).
  • Each seller's sales against target, days to collect and customers not reordered are counted.
  • Medicines about to run out are listed for buying.
  • The customer gets an email at every step of the order.

2. What Management no longer has to check by hand

The system checks these on every order and shows only what is wrong:

  • Price below the list price.
  • Price different from the last price given to that customer.
  • Not enough stock, and which warehouse has it.
  • Customer's licence expired.
  • Customer owes money past due.
  • Order takes the customer over their credit limit.
  • New customer not checked yet.
  • Prescription or papers missing.
  • Order under the wrong Division (the system does not allow it).

Management only reads the papers and presses Approve, Send back or Hold.

3. Everyone has their own screen

  • Every person has one login and sees only their own work.
  • Sellers: their orders, customers to call, visits, collections and their score.
  • Team leads: their team's orders, sales, attendance and approvals.
  • Management, Finance and Dispatch: their own desk with the orders waiting for them.
  • Online desk: every enquiry as one case, from quotation to payment.
  • Inventory: medicines, stock, buying and vendors.
  • HR: people, attendance, leave and payroll.
  • CEO: sales, money, orders, profit and people on one Home page.

4. Everyone's time is recorded

  • Start my day and End my day: time in and time out for everyone. Late and absent are counted.
  • Every order step records who did it and at what time: placed, approved, Finance checked, packed, shipped, delivered, paid.
  • The CEO sees how long each desk (Management, Finance, Dispatch) takes on an order.
  • Orders stuck for more than 2 days are flagged.
  • Online desk: every customer message must be answered within 15 minutes. A late reply shows in red.
  • Field visits: check-in time at each visit.

5. Other changes

  • Nothing is deleted. Orders are cancelled and goods are returned, so the history stays.
  • Messages inside the system replace emails between departments.
  • Stock, buying and bills are handled in the system, no longer in Zoho.
