Bishnoi One: before and now

5 tools replaced by one: the hub, Zoho, the old CRM, the Excel tracker and SEFMED. 133 seats, each with its own login.

BeforeNow
Target vs achievedHeads made the Excel by hand each evening.Live from the invoices, for any day, in one click, with totals for every team, channel and head.
Approving an orderManagement checked price, stock, licence, dues and credit by hand on every order.The system runs 9 checks on every order and shows only the problems. Management just decides.
ReordersReps remembered (or forgot) who was due.The system learns each customer's buying rhythm and tells the rep whom to call before they run out.
Where an order isCalls and messages to ask.Every step is recorded with who did it and when. Orders stuck for 2 days are flagged, and the CEO sees how long each desk takes.
Money owedChased from Zoho reports.Overdue is counted by each customer's terms. Credit limits are enforced, and every rep sees what their customers owe.
Stock and expiryZoho, with no say on which batch goes out.The nearest expiry ships first, so less goes to waste. On a recall, one search shows every customer the batch reached.
Online enquiriesChats, quotes and orders were scattered.One case from first message to refill. A 15-minute reply clock shows late replies in red.
People's timeSEFMED and manual registers.Everyone's time in and out, late and absent, and field check-ins, all in one place.
CEO's viewSeveral reports from several tools.One live screen: sales, collected, owed, gross profit, orders and who's in today.
Trust in the dataEntries could be edited or deleted quietly.Nothing is deleted and every change keeps its history. The system checks its own books against itself.
